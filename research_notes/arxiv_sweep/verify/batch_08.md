## 2512.02010 — Four Over Six: More Accurate NVFP4 Quantization with Adaptive Block Scaling

- **Exists:** yes. Cook, Guo, Xiao, Lin, Wyss, Nazemi, Mishra, del Mundo, Blankevoort, Han (MIT Han Lab / NVIDIA). Published 2025-12-01, v2 2026-05-09. Comment: "10 pages, 4 figures". No journal_ref; WebSearch found no venue → preprint/unknown.
- **Report claim (line 135, tag [S]):** "스케일 포맷 제안들(... Four-over-Six [2512.02010] ...)은 모두 실험 기반이다." Line 131 calls it "Four-over-Six 휴리스틱".
- **Verdict: partially_confirmed.**
  - "실험 기반" — confirmed. No theorem/lemma/closed form anywhere (grep for theorem/lemma/proposition/closed-form/gaussian: none). The mechanism is motivated by a worked example (Table 1: block [10,20,30,40], 30 scaled to 4.62 → rounded to 4, 13.4% rel. error; scaled to max=4 it is exact) and supported empirically by a threshold sweep on Llama-3.1-8B (Sec 2.2, l.236-255): "performance degrades steadily up until values greater than 4 are quantized, and then rapidly degrades after that, indicating that error due to rounding values around 5 is most responsible ... Smaller spikes ... around 2.5 and 3.5."
  - "스케일 포맷 제안" — NOT accurate. 4/6 does not propose a scale *format*: the E4M3 block scale and FP32 tensor scale of NVFP4 are kept. It is a block-scaling *algorithm*: per block, choose whether the block max maps to FP4 value 4 or 6, selected by MSE (PTQ, Table 4 / Sec 3.1) or MAE (pre-training, Fig 5 / App A.1). The only scale-related tweak is replacing M_FP8=448 by 256 in the tensor-scale Eq. (1) so that a scale of 4 remains selectable (l.651-657).
- **Correction:** line 135 should read e.g. "스케일링 규칙/포맷 제안들(UE5M3 [스케일 포맷], Four-over-Six [블록 스케일 선택 알고리즘, E4M3 스케일 유지], HiFloat4 [계층적 스케일 포맷, 원 제안은 2602.11287])은 모두 실험 기반이다." "휴리스틱" (line 131) is fine.
- **Numbers to know:** pre-training on Nemotron 3 Nano 30B-A3B (hybrid Mamba-Transformer MoE), 1T tokens, 384 B200, WSD schedule, NVIDIA NVFP4 recipe (SR on grads, RHT on WGRAD inputs, 2D block weight quant): "4/6 improves performance compared to the NVFP4 baseline, bringing training loss 13.0% closer to the BF16 baseline" (Sec 4.1, l.715-717). Applying 4/6 to activations only gives comparable performance (Fig 8). Kernel overhead "under 15%" of the NVFP4 quantization operation (l.668-669). PTQ: improves GPTQ/AWQ/SmoothQuant (Table 5).
- **Key quotes:** "Unlike integer formats, floating point formats have non-uniform step sizes which create larger quantization error on larger values. 4/6 takes advantage of this by adaptively scaling some blocks to smaller FP4 values" (abstract). "most of the quantization error in these formats comes from near-maximal values, as demonstrated in Section 2.2" (l.847-848).
- **Relevance to Idea 3:** The paper's own Sec 2.2 finding (error concentrated at scaled values ~5, 2.5, 3.5, i.e. the wide E2M1 cells) is exactly what the report's Lloyd–Max/extreme-value framing would predict; the report could cite it as empirical support rather than only as a "heuristic". The HiF4 paper (2604.08826, Table 1 caption) labels 4/6 "Quartet II's 4/6 forward scale selection" — Quartet II (2601.22813) apparently reuses it.

## 2604.08826 — HiFloat4 Format for Language Model Pre-training on Ascend NPUs

- **Exists:** yes. Taghian et al. (25 authors, Huawei). Published 2026-04-09, updated 2026-09-25. No comment, no journal_ref; WebSearch found no venue → preprint/unknown. (A different ACL 2026 Industry paper covers HiFloat *inference* evaluation; not this paper.)
- **Report claim (line 135, [S]):** HiFloat4 [2604.08826] is a "스케일 포맷 제안" and "실험 기반". Line 161 says the idea "하드웨어 포맷 논쟁(UE5M3, HiFloat4)에 직접 개입한다."
- **Verdict: partially_confirmed.**
  - "실험 기반" — confirmed. Purely empirical (ablations, Table 3, Fig 3-4); no theoretical analysis of the format.
  - "포맷 제안" attributed to 2604.08826 — incorrect attribution. The paper says explicitly: "We do not claim the format itself, which was introduced for inference; what is new is that its hierarchical scaling suffices for stable pretraining" (l.92-95). The format was proposed in Luo et al. 2026, "HiFloat4 format for language model inference", arXiv:2602.11287 (ref. l.814-816).
- **Correction:** cite 2602.11287 for the HiFloat4 format proposal and describe 2604.08826 as "HiF4 사전학습 연구 (emulated, 50B tokens)". If the report wants to keep 2604.08826 as the format description, note it is secondhand (Appendix B.2 refers to Luo et al. for design rationale).
- **What HiF4 is (App. B.2, l.947-966; Fig 1c):** block of 64 elements; level-1 shared unsigned E6M2 scale (bias 48, implicit leading 1); level-2 and level-3 1-bit micro-exponents over 8-way and 16-way partitions, each level-3 exponent governing 4 contiguous elements; elements in S1P2 = **E1M2 (uniform grid)**; 32 bits metadata / 64 elements = 0.5 bit/value → 4.5 bits/value, storage-matched to NVFP4 (MXFP4 = 4.25). Dynamic range: "NVFP4 covers 22 binades against HiF4's 69" (l.704).
- **Numbers (Table 3, 50B tokens, each format's best config: RHT for HiF4; TF+SR+RHT for MXFP4; PTS+SR+RHT for NVFP4; relative loss vs BF16):** OpenPangu-1B MXFP4 1.75% / NVFP4 1.26% / HiF4 1.25%; Llama3-8B 1.44 / 0.99 / 0.85; Qwen3-MoE-30B 1.55 / 0.99 / 0.88. Ablation Fig 4a (OpenPangu-1B): HiF4 no stabilizer 1.55%, +RHT 1.19%; +SR hurts (+4.56% alone); MXFP4 full stack 1.79%; NVFP4 PTS-only 2.00%, PTS+RHT 1.42%, PTS+RHT+SR 1.27%; NVFP4 without PTS diverges in every SR/RHT combination. Qwen3-MoE-30B: HiF4 no stabilizer 1.87% vs NVFP4+PTS 3.29%. (Minor internal inconsistency: text l.446 says HiF4 1B = 1.19% while Table 3 lists 1.25%.)
- **Limitations stated (l.741-754):** all FP4 arithmetic emulated ("no Ascend NPU supports HiF4 or MXFP4 natively yet, so every GEMM executes in BF16 with quantization simulated around it"); fixed 50B tokens, "nothing here speaks to convergence over long horizons"; only OpenPangu-1B multi-seed; "0.08–0.14 point HiF4–NVFP4 margins are not separable from run-to-run variation on this evidence, which is why the paper rests on the stabilization results instead." No area/power/throughput measured (l.329-330).
- **Relevance to Idea 3:** HiF4 uses a *uniform* E1M2 element grid with hierarchical power-of-two micro-scales — it is a live data point on exactly the (block size, element grid, scale format) axes of the proposed theory, and its "SR hurts HiF4 / helps MXFP4" finding (l.282-291) is a rounding-direction × grid interaction the theory should predict. Also note UFP4 (below) independently argues for E1M2.
- **Side note (outside this batch):** report line 234 writes "HiFloat4/Rollout-ResQ ... [2607.26515]"; that label looks like a conflation with this paper's name and should be checked by whoever verifies 2607.26515.

## 2606.20381 — Rethinking Shrinkage Bias in LLM FP4 Pretraining: Geometric Origin, Systemic Impact, and UFP4 Recipe

- **Exists:** yes. Zhao, Chen, Tian, ... Zhang, Zhou (Ant Group, Ling Team). Published 2026-06-18 (v1 only). Comment "18 pages, 12 figures". No venue → preprint/unknown.
- **Report claims:**
  1. (line 136, [S]) UFP4 claims "E2M1의 RTNE가 음의 기대 라운딩 오차(shrinkage bias)를 가지며 RHT가 이를 증폭한다" — **confirmed** verbatim. Abstract: "non-uniform formats such as E2M1 inherently suffer from Shrinkage Bias, a systematic negative rounding error caused by the geometric asymmetry of their representable bins. We show that this bias accumulates multiplicatively across layers and is amplified by the Random Hadamard Transform (RHT)". Sec 3.1 defines it as "a negative expected Round-to-Nearest-Even (RTNE) error in the normalized magnitude space".
  2. (line 136) "자동 리뷰는 효과 분리가 안 됐다고 지적했다" — **not checkable** (refers to an external automated review, not to the paper).
  3. (line 136) "정량 이론이 없다" — **overstated / partially incorrect.** The paper does contain a quantitative bin-level derivation: Eq. (2) (l.299-306): under locally uniform density within bin B_i=((q_{i-1}+q_i)/2,(q_i+q_{i+1})/2), E[ρ_G(t)−t | t∈B_i] = (ℓ_i − r_i)/2 = (2q_i − q_{i−1} − q_{i+1})/4; worked example (footnote 2, l.417): q_i=2, bin (1.75, 2.5), ℓ=0.25, r=0.5 → −0.125; q_i=4 "the same asymmetry at double the scale"; uniform grids have ℓ_i=r_i → zero. Propagation: Eq. (3)-(5) (l.345-402): projection coefficient α_A = ⟨A,Â⟩_F/‖A‖²_F, exact GEMM identity Zq = α_Aα_B Z + residuals, attenuation η≈α_Aα_B<1, and ∏η_k = ∏(1−δ_k) ≈ exp(−Σδ_k) (Appendix C, l.2317-2410). What is genuinely absent: (a) any distribution-level closed form — no Gaussian or other block-distribution assumption appears anywhere (grep "gaussian" = 0 hits); (b) no dependence on block size or scale format (scale hierarchy is "orthogonal", l.660); (c) the "RHT amplifies" claim is argued qualitatively ("RHT transforms the tensor from being dynamic-range-limited to local-resolution-limited", l.405-410) and supported empirically on real tensors via effective bucket ratio B_eff (Eq. 6) and ΔSQNR (Eq. 7) in Fig 2, not derived. The report's own numbers (line 150: −0.66%, 0.9968) are not in the paper and are correctly labelled as the report's quantification.
  4. (line 172, Idea 4) "UFP4는 shrinkage bias [를 병목으로 주장]" — **confirmed**: "providing a unified explanation for the training instability observed in existing E2M1-based FP4 recipes" (abstract; contributions l.176-178).
- **Verdict: partially_confirmed.**
- **Correction (line 136):** replace "정량 이론이 없다" with e.g. "bin 수준 조건부 기대오차 식(Eq. 2: (ℓ_i−r_i)/2, q=2 bin에서 −0.125)과 승법 누적 항등식(Eq. 4–5)은 제시하지만, 블록 분포(예: Gaussian) 가정 하의 닫힌 형태 기대편향, 블록 크기·스케일 포맷 의존성, RHT 증폭의 해석적 유도는 없다 (RHT 효과는 실제 텐서의 B_eff/ΔSQNR로만 경험적으로 보임)."
- **Numbers:** long-run BF16-relative LM loss error |L_r−L_BF16|/L_BF16 (latest-1000-step; Sec 5.3, l.1342-1346): Dense 1.5B 1.2570% → 0.9673%; MoE 7.9B 2.3596% → 1.8469%; MoE 124B 1.7308% → 1.3863% (E2M1 reference → UFP4). Recipe (Table 1, l.669-680): quant block 1×16, scale hierarchy "FP32 single-level" (i.e. NOT NVFP4's E4M3 block scale — both the E2M1 reference and UFP4 use FP32 block scales), RHT block 16, SR on dY only, no 2D weight scaling; E2M1 reference applies RHT to bwd_dw only, UFP4 to fwd_y, bwd_dx, bwd_dw. RHT-scope ablation at >100B tokens (Table 2).
- **Caveat for the report:** because UFP4's comparison uses FP32 block scales, its result does not isolate the E2M1-vs-E1M2 element grid from scale-format effects under real NVFP4 (E4M3) scaling; the "E2M1 reference" is not the NVIDIA NVFP4 recipe. Also the paper's own RHT analysis (Fig 2, l.576-580) says RHT "shift[s] tensor mass from extreme tails into mid-magnitude regions ... into E2M1's most asymmetric rounding bins" — this is the mechanism the report should cite for "RHT 증폭".

## 2501.02423 — Scaling Laws for Floating Point Quantization Training

- **Exists:** yes. Sun, Li, Xie, Han, Wu, Yang, ... Wang, Jiang (Tencent Hunyuan, Univ. of Macau, CUHK, Institute of Science Tokyo). Published 2025-01-05, v3 2025-06-04.
- **Venue:** ICML 2025 — header l.79-81: "Proceedings of the 42nd International Conference on Machine Learning, Vancouver, Canada. PMLR 267, 2025."
- **Report claim (line 137, tag [U: 내용 미확인]):** "Sun et al.의 FP 스케일링 법칙은 (E, M, B)에 대한 적합식이다."
- **Verdict: confirmed.** The unified ("Capybara") scaling law, Eq. (16)/(17)/(26) (l.195-197, 763-805, 1532-1536):
  L(N, D, E, M, B) = n/N^α + d/D^β + ε + (D^β / N^α) · log₂B / (γ (E+0.5)^δ (M+0.5)^ν),
  with E = exponent bits, M = mantissa bits, B = block size of the scaling factor, N = params, D = tokens. Fitted constants (Table 2, l.1546-1568): n=69.2343, α=0.2368, d=68973.0621, β=0.5162, ε=1.9061, γ=11334.5197, δ=3.1926, ν=2.9543. Fitted on "all above 358 experiments in Section 3 containing various N, D, E, M, B settings" (l.812), model sizes 41M–679M, validated on 1.2B/7B/70B (l.883).
- **Precision for the report:** it is a fit over (N, D, E, M, B), not (E, M, B) alone; the low-precision penalty term ρ(E,M,B) is multiplied by D^β/N^α ("knowledge intensity"). Tag can move from [U] to [V]. Suggested wording: "Sun et al.(ICML 2025)의 통합 스케일링 법칙은 L(N,D,E,M,B)에 대한 경험적 적합식으로, 저정밀 페널티 항 (D^β/N^α)·log₂B/(γ(E+0.5)^δ(M+0.5)^ν)을 Chinchilla 손실에 더한 형태다 (358 runs, 41M–679M)."
- **Other claims in the paper the report may use:** exponent bits "contribute slightly more to the model performance than mantissa bits" (abstract); existence of a critical data size D_crit beyond which more data hurts under low precision (Sec 4.3, Eq. 21); "best cost-performance precision should lie between 4-8 bits" (abstract, l.53, l.179). Channel-wise scaling has an equivalent log₂B ≈ 13.1567 (l.683). Note the law is purely empirical: block size enters only through log₂B with no format-geometry term, which is exactly the gap Idea 3 targets.
