## A. Full-text verified (theorem + assumptions)

### 1. 2607.28699 WitCert (Wei et al., v3 2026-08-16; Lean proofs) — ideas 1, 7, 9
- **Theorem 1 (proved).** If |ε_t| ≤ c_t for every cached token, then TV(p, p̃) ≤ ½(A² − 1) with A = E_p̃[e^c]. This is worst case and first order in c.
- **Lemma 1 / Theorem 2 (proved).** RoPE acts unitarily within each frequency band, so the per-band residual norms w_{t,b} are invariant to query position. This gives |ε_t| ≤ (1/√d) Σ_b ‖q_b‖ w_{t,b} for any cache-preserving quantizer and any query, adaptive queries included.
- **Lemma 2 (proved).** A subtractive-dither residual is sub-Gaussian with proxy s²/12, which equals its variance. The probabilistic certificate holds only for non-adaptive queries (prefill or teacher-forced). The authors state this explicitly.
- **Appendix Proposition 2, "rigidity" (proved).** Every RoPE-equivariant static linear sketch is a frequency selection with per-frequency complex scalars. When r < p, whole frequency pairs must be dropped.
- **Empirical.** The authors' own earlier attention-weighted bound was **wrong**: in a minimal counterexample it underestimates TV by 7.8x, yet it had passed 32,256 checks in aggregate. The Tier-A bound is vacuous by orders of magnitude for 1–2-bit schemes and informative for 8-bit ones. In a 28-layer sweep, no single layer's pollution loses anything (0/28): aggressive schemes survive on cross-layer cancellation. Gating restores raw fp8 on hard RULER from 22.8 to 79.7.
- **Impact.** The report's §0.2(4) says no theorem addresses attention-output error. That is no longer true for worst-case TV. Idea 1's second-order *signed bias* is still absent from WitCert, whose bound has no sign and no ½Var term. Idea 9's RoPE pair-mixing lemma now has partial prior art (band unitarity plus rigidity). Idea 7 should note that subtractive dither is already used as a certification device.

### 2. 2606.06521 P-Cast FP8 attention (Lau, 2026-06-02; 8-page technical note) — ideas 1, 3
- **Proposition 1 / Corollary 2 (leading order).** Assume non-sink scores ~ N(0,1), a single sink of strength Δ, forward KV iteration, and E4M3 P-cast with static scale S. The fraction of non-sink P values that underflow to zero is then F = Φ(Δ + δ_k − 6.93 − ln S), where 6.93 = 10 ln 2 and δ_4 ≈ 1.0 is measured. The collapse threshold is Δ_c = 6.93 + ln S − δ_k.
- **Theorem 4.** Reverse iteration plus S = 256 guarantees zero underflow: Pr < Φ(−7.2) < 1e−12.
- **Theorem 7.** S = 2^8 is characterized as bit-exact, the lower envelope of dp(S), and maximal normal-range coverage among powers of two. The non-bit-exact S = 448 covers slightly more.
- **Empirical.** 3–10x lower MSE at moderate sink strength. Q, K and V are kept in FP32 to isolate the cast. V is assumed pairwise uncorrelated. The analysis covers a single sink only.
- **Impact.** This sink-induced error is a *deterministic negative bias* on non-sink mass. Idea 1's "sink amplification" story is about stochastic second-order bias, so idea 1 must separate the two mechanisms or cite this one.

### 3. 2607.23390 "When Can Depth Replace Precision?" (Soltanalian, 141 pages, Lean core) — idea 2
- **Proved.** Fixed-grid full-state write-back has error envelope E∞ + C/D + Dρ_z, so there is a finite optimal depth √(C/ρ_z). In the scalar recursion x_{k+1} = Q_Δ(x_k + 1/D), the state stays at 0 whenever D > 2/Δ. This is exactly idea 2's deadband, for a = 1.
- **Theorem 8 (proved).** With increment error feedback (a carry r_k), max_k ‖ẑ_k − z_k‖ ≤ ρ e^{L_z T} + η_G Φ_{L_z}(T). There is also a common-lattice conservation law, and a converse D = Θ(L).
- **Key sentence.** "Precision is relocated into a persistent carry or compensated accumulator; it is not eliminated."
- **Impact.** The deadband no-go and the error-feedback carry for neural recurrences now have prior art, which idea 2 must cite. The bound grows as e^{LT}, so idea 2's uniform-in-T bound for contractive a < 1 is still new. The serious new objection is that the carry has the dimension of the state. Idea 2 must account for carry bits, or the memory savings disappear.

### 4. 2606.03458 KVarN (Huawei, 2026-06-02) — ideas 1, 9
- **Empirical only.** Under KIVI 2-bit, the largest K errors are mostly token-*magnitude* (norm) errors rather than direction errors (Fig. 1a, using the Eq. 3 decomposition). More than 98% of the top-5% errors lie in K (Llama3.1-8B, footnote 3). Errors accumulate across layers and decode steps in "pseudo-decode", which a prefill-style evaluation hides. The fix is Hadamard followed by Sinkhorn-style variance normalization on both axes.
- **Impact.** A token-norm error multiplies the whole logit s_t, so the noise is score-correlated and heteroscedastic. That is exactly the regime of idea 1's bias, and it gives idea 1 a concrete noise model. It also weakens "K must be per-channel", because per-channel K is what creates the token-norm errors.

### 5. 2607.18745 Contraction-Gauge Preconditioning (Sao et al., ORNL, 50 pages) — ideas 10, 1/9, 7
- **Theorem 3.3.** An exact identity: E‖ÂB̂ − AB‖_F² = Σ v^A_ik‖B_k‖² + Σ v^B_kj‖A_:k‖² + Σ v^A v^B. It holds exactly for non-overloading subtractive dither and for independent stochastic rounding. For RTN it is only empirical: rank correlation 0.937 at 8 bits and 0.918 at 4 bits.
- **Theorem 9.1.** The optimal split of a fixed total bit sum between the two operands has gap ½log2(P_A/P_B). In their example P_A/P_B ≈ 11 predicts 1.7 bits, and a 9/7 split beats 8/8 by 1.7x.
- **§6.** A Haar rotation of a flat row raises the expected coherence from 1 to Θ(log K), so rotation can hurt.
- **Theorems 4.2 / Proposition 4.3.** A geometric program finds the globally optimal diagonal fold, and a linear program checks whether the identity fold is already optimal.
- **Impact.** This is a ready-made allocation rule for idea 10 (latent versus absorbed factors) and for the K/V bit split. It gives theoretical support for the KVLinC observation that a K-Hadamard is harmful per-channel.

### 6. 2607.24377 MXAttention (Huawei MindIE, 2026-07-27) — idea 3
- **Theorem 1.** For any absolutely continuous law of log2(block max), J(q) = E[D(X_q)] is minimized at q* = 29/4 = 7.25 under ceiling-based E8M0 scaling. The proof uses log-periodicity: g(q/2) = 2g(q).
- **Hidden assumption.** D(x) is defined as the MSE of a variable uniform on [0, x] (Eq. 21–22). "Distribution-independent" therefore holds only with respect to the block-max law. Within a block, elements are assumed uniform on [0, M]. Block size and element distribution do not enter.
- **Impact.** This directly overlaps idea 3's "closed-form distortion" goal and supplies the log-periodic wrapping trick. Idea 3's distinctive part, the extreme-value effect of block size and element law, is untouched and falsifiable against it.

## B. Abstract-level (claims as stated; not verified in full text)
- **2606.09012** (QAT basin). Proves finite-time recovery of STE-QAT under local quantizer-compatibility assumptions. PTQ fails geometrically when the grid is comparable to the basin width ("river/valley" model). Ideas 4 and 5.
- **2606.06934** (stability of fixed-point GD/SGD). RTN makes GD's generalization error O(T/√n) instead of O(T/n), with matching lower bounds. Uniform stability becomes Ω(T). SGD with RTN gets O(T/n) in 1-D and O(T²/n) in higher dimensions. Stochastic rounding can add generalization error that grows with dimension. Ideas 4 and 3; this contradicts the folklore that SR is always better.
- **2607.12266** (coverage model). At W4A4, 85–93% of the variance of f(S) is explained by per-layer effects. The coverage model is f(S) = c(1 − Π(1 − a_i)). The additive model is the optimal first-order predictor, with a Parseval certificate. Tested at 30B–355B. Bears on the report's §0.2(1) HIGGS bridge.
- **2606.28432** (Fisher spectral perturbation). Theorem 4.3: λ_max of the empirical FIM under quantization noise is at least its unperturbed value up to a third-order remainder, and strictly larger at leading order. A threshold inflated 244x is a single measurement. Idea 8 (weak).
- **2606.22249** (expressive power). Claims 1.58 bits is the limiting weight precision (universal approximation versus expressive collapse) and that degradation is polynomial in bits. Abstract only, and suspicious.
- **2606.15682** ReQAT (ICML 2026) and 2606.13233 ReSET (already cited in the report). FP4 failures **concentrate on low-entropy tokens** (digits, operators). This contradicts idea 8's weight w_t = 1 − ‖p_t‖², which down-weights exactly those tokens.
- **2607.15810** QUADS. NVFP4 rollout with BF16 training collapses after about 150 steps. Activation error, not weight error, dominates, because weights can share one quantize-dequantize path. Reports +21.49 pass@1. Idea 6.
- **2607.24062** ACRL. Keeping the training–inference discrepancy within a controlled band raises policy entropy and beats IS fixes under FP8 rollout. A direct competitor to idea 6.
- **2607.16237** (recursive reasoning). Per-tensor 4-bit causes systematic bias accumulation through weight-tied recursion: Sudoku drops from 84.1% to 0.0%. Per-block scaling fully restores it, so granularity matters more than bit-width. Ideas 2 and 3.
- **2607.14630** (cross-layer error compensation). Proves that e_{l+1} = A_l e_l + q_l is exact when A_l is a finite difference. At 1.125 bits on Qwen2.5-1.5B, the PPL ratio is 9.56 versus 14.09 for logit distillation. Idea 5 and §0.2(1).
- **2607.00908** TASA. "Perplexity illusion": Kendall τ ≈ 0 between PPL-sensitive layers and reasoning-sensitive layers. Calibrating on the target task alone can hurt. Ideas 5 and 8.
- **2607.20757** GaugeQuant. A LogSumExp symmetry-breaking term during training learns rotations without any calibration data. LLaMA-2-7B W4A4 g128 PPL goes from 8.22 to 6.73. A counterpoint to idea 5's overfitting story.
- **2607.08779** (signed symmetric quantization). The signed absmax grid is conditionally bound-optimal for ℓ2 error, and the condition holds for 88–99% of LLM weight groups. Negating the scale is equivalent to a unit zero-point shift. Idea 3.
- **2608.11212** (MoE route flips). About 31% of the damage from 4-bit KV on OLMoE is mediated by routing. The router margin detects that a flip happened (AUC 0.772), but the sign of its harm is at chance. A new direction, the "cell boundary" analogue for routers.
- **2607.11317** (e-CUSUM). log p(w_t) + H_t is a mean-zero martingale under the model's own sampling law, so it is the wrong signal for a monitor. INT4 accuracy goes from 63% to 69%, but p = 0.18. Ideas 6 and 8.
- **2607.27275** (flat score). On τ²-bench, 4-bit weights amplify existing agent failures by up to 2.5x but create no new ones (0.18%). The ten-error budget masks this. Evaluation hygiene (appendix B).
