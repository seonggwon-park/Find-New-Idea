Simulation scripts: `scratchpad/h07/sim.py` and `sim2.py`. Each uses 2^21 iid elements, E2M1 RTN with saturation at 6, and E4M3 RTN (3-bit mantissa, subnormals, max 448).

## Verdict
- Scores: novelty 2/5, provability 3/5, feasibility 5/5.
- **Recommendation: fold_into_existing (idea 3).** The three predictions came out as follows on a CPU check:
  - (i) depends on an encoding the paper does not state;
  - (ii) is reproduced numerically;
  - (iii) holds, but it is a small effect.
- The machinery is already published: Fasoli 2601.19026 (exact block-max density × E2M1 cell integrals, including the deadzone) and Soloveychik 2210.05470 (Gumbel, power-of-two round-up scale). What H8 adds is three application remarks, not a separate paper.

## Interpretation
- **(i) is not a prediction. It is a test of which scale encoding was used.**
  - An E4M3 block scale lets FP4 block-16 beat the mantissa-only figure, because absmax scaling puts the block max near 6 with a fine scale. The gap then shrinks to about 11 dB.
  - A power-of-two scale costs about 1.5-3 dB and gives 12.6-14.0 dB.
  - With an NVFP4-style scale, the 1.2 dB excess must come from something an iid within-block law lacks:
    - channel-outlier structure in activations (one massive channel per block drives the others into the deadzone, which gives an integer-like floor);
    - the non-multiplicative φ entering KBBQ's κ normalisation.
  - In that case H8(i) is falsified under its own criterion (miss > 0.5 dB).
- **(ii) is real but modest.**
  - Under the 2^U model (U uniform on [0,1), γ = 2^U ≥ 1) the cosine is a 2-D integral over (U, y), where y is the within-block normalised magnitude from the block-max-conditioned law. It is semi-closed form and not truly closed form.
  - It is close to constant only for B ≥ 16 and near-Gaussian data. It is not exactly independent of B.
  - Li's "tight over 18,876 tensors" agrees with a Gaussian-like law at B = 32.
- **(iii) is correct but low-impact.**
  - UOS is optimal for its own proxy. Under real within-block laws, q* grows with B and with tail weight because more mass sits in the deadzone, which a smaller scale rescues.
  - The Gaussian B = 32 coincidence actually supports UOS at its operating point.

## Math sketch
- Block: iid |x| with density f and CDF F; M = max. M has density B·f(m)F(m)^{B−1}, which is exact (Fasoli eq. 5). Its Gumbel asymptotic is M ≈ σ(√(2 ln 2B) − …).
- Given M, the other B−1 elements are iid f truncated to [0, M]. Write y = x/M ∈ [0, 1] with conditional density h_B(y | M).
- **(ii):** ceiling scale s = s*·γ with s* = M/6 and γ = 2^{U}, where U = ⌈log2(M/6)⌉ − log2(M/6) is wrapped uniform under a log-smooth law of M.
  - Write z = 6y. Then e_scale = s*(γ R(z/γ) − R(z)) and e_grid = s*(R(z) − z)·1[z ≥ 0.25].
  - cos = E_{U,M}[Σ e_s e_g] / √(E[Σ e_s²] E[Σ e_g²]): a 2-D integral (U, z) once M is integrated into h_B.
  - Claim to prove: as B → ∞, with h_B for a Gaussian parent, the cosine converges to a limit c_∞(f). Its B-dependence is O(1/ln B), from the change in deadzone mass.
- **(iii):** J_true(q; B, f) = E_M E_{U}[ Σ_i (x_i − sΠ(x_i/s))² ], with s = 2^{⌈log2(M/q)⌉}.
  - The log-periodicity argument of MXAttention still removes the law of log2 M. But the conditional element law in units of M is h_B(·), not uniform, so the stationarity condition becomes D_{h_B}(q) = D_{h_B}(q/2)·(appropriate factor).
  - q*(B, f) is the root of a 1-D equation. A provable statement: q* is nondecreasing in B whenever h_B places increasing mass near 0 (stochastic ordering).
- **(i):** the elementwise SNR difference equals C_fp8 − C_fp4(scale encoding, f, B). Only the mantissa term is format-generic. The E4M3-scale and UE8M0-scale cases differ by E[γ² …] (Li: E[γ] = 1/ln 2, E[γ²] = 3/(2 ln 2)).

## Decisive experiment (CPU plus about 1 GPU-day)
1. Re-run KBBQ's noise-law cells (or a subset: Llama-3.2-1B and Qwen3-4B, four GEMMs × blocks) under three block-16 scale encodings: E4M3 (NVFP4), UE8M0 ceiling, and FP32 exact. Report the intercept gap for each.
   - Falsification: if the E4M3 encoding still yields about 13.2 dB, the iid EVT model is wrong for (i). The excess then needs an outlier-channel mixture term, which must be estimated from real tensors (fraction of blocks whose max exceeds 4× the block RMS).
2. Compute cos(e_scale, e_grid) on Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen3-8B weights for B ∈ {8, 16, 32, 64, 128} against the 2-D integral computed with the empirical within-block law.
   - Falsification: deviation > 0.03, or |dcos/dlog2 B| > 0.03 for B ≥ 16.
3. Compute q*(B) on real post-Hadamard Q/K/V tensors and weights against the predicted curve.
   - Falsification: real q* stays at 7.25 ± 0.125 for B = 128.
