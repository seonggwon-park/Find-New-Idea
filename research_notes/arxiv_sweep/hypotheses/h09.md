## Verdict
- Scores: novelty 2/5, provability 2/5, feasibility 4/5.
- **Recommendation: fold_into_existing (idea 8).** H10 is the mechanism-separation experiment that the idea-8 judge already asked for (judge/idea8.md, "새 가설" 1 and 3). LAQuant names the constructive version as its own future work. As written, the test has two design flaws that make it unable to kill anything:
  1. **No on-policy arm.** AYOT Table 4 shows a large self-generated vs external-trace gap even when both are in-domain.
  2. **Top-k principal-angle cosine is the wrong matching statistic.** GPTQ's mismatch loss depends on eigenvalues too, i.e. tr(H_eval H_cal^{-1}) as in TASA Thm 1, not only on top-k subspaces.

  Both are fixed in the sharpened statement below.
- The extra arm (exact hidden-space Fisher trace) is the only piece with no published counterpart. The identity behind it is one line, so it is an arm inside idea 8, not a paper.

## Theorist: what can be stated and proved

**Fact 0 (structural; what makes the dichotomy partly false).** Sequential GPTQ sees calibration data only through the per-layer second moments H_ℓ = Σ_t w_t x_t x_tᵀ, with x_t computed through the already-quantized prefix. Any "entropy-profile" effect must therefore act through H_ℓ: either via which tokens are present or via the weights w_t. "Subspace vs entropy" really asks *which H_ℓ the deployment loss rewards*:
- H_eval^unif = E_eval[x xᵀ], or
- H_eval^F = E_eval[φ_t x xᵀ] with a Fisher factor φ_t.

**Proposition A (mismatch loss; standard, cf. TASA Thm 1).** Assume:
- the weights stay in the unconstrained quadratic regime;
- GPTQ's error ΔW_cal has an OBS-type second moment E[ΔWᵀΔW] ∝ H_cal^{-1} (high-rate rounding, isotropic per-row noise).

Then the expected deployment proxy loss is E tr(ΔW H_eval ΔWᵀ) ∝ tr(H_eval H_cal^{-1}). It depends on eigenvalues as well as subspaces. c̄_k discards the spectrum and treats the k directions equally. Two corpora with the same c̄_k can therefore differ arbitrarily in this loss: rescale a trailing eigenvalue of H_cal toward 0 while leaving the top-k subspace fixed, and the loss diverges, which is exactly TASA's α→0 limit. **Sharpening:** select the subspace-matched C4 subset by minimizing tr(H_eval H_cal^{-1}) (or a ridge version), not by maximizing c̄_k. A cheap per-document surrogate is the captured-energy score ‖P_eval^k x‖²/‖x‖², which is additive over tokens and makes greedy selection O(N).

**Lemma B (hidden-space Fisher trace; exact, one line).** Let h be the input to the unembedding U ∈ R^{V×d}, with no final norm in between, and p = softmax(Uh). Then F_h = Uᵀ(diag p − ppᵀ)U and
 tr F_h = Σ_i p_i‖u_i‖² − ‖Σ_i p_i u_i‖² = E_{i∼p}‖u_i − ū‖², i.e. the variance of the unembedding rows under p.
 Also E_{y∼p}‖∇_h ℓ(y)‖² = tr F_h. So on *self-generated traces sampled at T=1*, the token weight of GuidedQuant with g=1 (scalar ‖∂ℓ/∂z_t‖² averaged over channels) has expectation tr(J_tᵀ F_h J_t)/d. The forward-only arm equals this when J_t = I.

 Assumptions that break exactness:
 - (a) the final RMSNorm Jacobian, diag(γ)/rms · (I − hhᵀ/‖h‖²), which is cheap to fold in exactly;
 - (b) for layer ℓ < L the residual-stream Jacobian J_{ℓ→L} ≠ I. This is the real approximation, and LAQuant App. C Table 6 measures 30-40× anisotropy for J;
 - (c) traces sampled at T=0.6, which bias the empirical Fisher;
 - (d) a scalar weight can at best match GuidedQuant with g=1, never its channel-grouped version.

 1−‖p‖² is the special case UUᵀ ∝ I. **The arm's claim must be restated as "closes the gap to GuidedQuant-g1", not to GuidedQuant.**

**What is not provable.** The claim "subspace-matched C4 recovers most of the AIME gain" is purely empirical. The second-order proxy → AIME accuracy link has no theorem (HIGGS-type linearity holds only at 3-8 bit, for PPL). Provability is 2: the two lemmas are textbook, and the interesting claims are empirical.

## Experimentalist: decisive design (about 1 GPU-week on H100)

Model: run at W3G128 GPTQ, where the effect is large. Use R1-Distill-Qwen-7B (2504.04823 Table 2 setting scaled up) or Qwen3-4B (LAQuant +38 pp). Add W4G128 only as a sanity check: at W4 the gap is small and unresolvable on AIME.

Stage 0 (CPU/1 GPU-hour), measure for every arm:
- c̄_64;
- tr(H_eval H_cal^{-1}) per layer, with H_eval from 64 held-out self-generated AIME/MATH traces;
- the token-entropy histogram;
- the mean NLL under the FP model (the on-policy-ness index).

This also settles idea-8 premise 3 (judge note): whether generic text has higher entropy than traces.

Arms (128×4096 tokens each, same token budget):
1. C4 random.
2. C4 selected by min tr(H_eval H_cal^{-1}) (greedy on the captured-energy surrogate), with its natural entropy profile.
3. Arm 2 re-sampled to match the trace entropy histogram (2×2 cell: subspace matched × entropy matched).
4. External R1 traces on the same problems (domain-matched, off-policy).
5. Self-generated traces (reference).
6. Arm 5 with w_t = max(ε, 1−‖p_t‖²), ε=0.01 (RSQ floor).
7. Arm 5 with w_t = tr F_h (Lemma B, RMSNorm folded).
8. Arm 5 with GuidedQuant g=1.
9. Optional: arm 2 with w_t from arm 7, to test whether weighting rescues generic data.

Evaluation:
- MATH-500 and AIME24+25, avg@16 at 32k tokens, with CoT length;
- teacher-forced KL on 64 held-out self traces (a low-variance proxy).

vLLM throughput is about 15M generated tokens per arm, roughly 1 GPU-h per arm on an H100, so 9 arms × 2 seeds of calibration sampling fit in about 2 days. GPTQ plus GuidedQuant cost under 1 h per arm.

Pre-registered outcome, with R(arm) = (Acc_arm − Acc_1)/(Acc_5 − Acc_1) and 95% CIs from a bootstrap over problems × samples:
- **Idea 8's entropy mechanism is dead** if R(2) ≥ 0.7, R(3) − R(2) ≤ 0.1, and (Acc_6 − Acc_5) is within CI.
- **Rescued** if R(2) ≤ 0.3 even though tr(H_eval H_cal^{-1}) for arm 2 is ≤ 1.2× that of arm 4, and R(3) − R(2) ≥ 0.2.
- **New finding, likely** (AYOT Table 4): R(4) ≪ 1 while c̄_k(4) ≈ c̄_k(5). Then neither subspace nor entropy weighting is the mechanism; on-policy-ness is, measured as the FP-model NLL of the calibration text.
- **Extra arm** succeeds if (Acc_7 − Acc_6) ≥ 0.5 (Acc_8 − Acc_6) and KL_7 ≤ KL_6 − 0.5 (KL_6 − KL_8).
- **Inconclusive guard:** if the best achievable C4 subset cannot bring tr(H_eval H_cal^{-1}) within 1.5× of arm 4, the subspace arm cannot reach the trace regime. Report it as a dose-response curve (accuracy vs the mismatch loss across nested subsets), not as a kill.

Main risks:
- C4 may not contain enough math to reach trace-level alignment, which gives a ceiling effect.
- AIME variance: 30 problems per year, hence avg@16 and the KL proxy.
- 2606.25519: calibration optimization stays "strictly below QAT", so a positive result is a mechanism result, not a SOTA result.

## Novelty hunt summary
- Subspace-matched generic subset: proposed as future work by LAQuant (l.4192). Activation-space curation in general form: COLA 2510.10618. Alignment ≠ quality: TASA 2607.00908, with tr(H_test H^{-1}) Thm 1.
- On-policy (self-generated) beats in-domain external CoT: AYOT 2608.01078 Table 4. This was not in H10 and is the most likely outcome driver.
- Token-weighted GPTQ: GuidedQuant 2505.07004 Sec. 3.2, RSQ 2503.01820. Entropy-weighting premise: idea-8 judge note.
- Not found anywhere: the exact hidden-space Fisher-trace weight as a forward-only calibration weight, and a 2×2 causal separation of subspace × entropy × on-policy for GPTQ on LRMs. That is a solid section of idea 8, not a standalone paper.
