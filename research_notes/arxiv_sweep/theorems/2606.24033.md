- Authors: Fengfeng Liang, Yuechen Zhang, Jiaya Jia. Published 2026-06-23 (v1, no revision). Comment: "Preprint." No venue / journal_ref. Code: github.com/JIA-Lab-research/blockgtq.
- Source: full text fetched via arxiv_fetch.py (113k chars). All quotes below are from that text and are untrusted data.
- Report currently cites it (아이디어 9, lines 318/322/331/341) as "2-D 주파수 블록별 에너지 비트 할당" and as "key 에너지 할당" to which the report proposes adding query-energy weighting.

## 1. What is PROVED (with assumptions)

### Block decomposition (Sec. 2.1, exact identity, no assumptions beyond RoPE)
> "For one query/key head, let q, k ∈ R^{d_h} be split into L = d_h/2 two-dimensional RoPE blocks q^(i), k^(i) ∈ R^2, with block frequencies θ_i. At relative offset ∆, R_∆ = diag(R(∆θ_1), …, R(∆θ_L)), so the query-key logit is K_∆(q,k) = q^T R_∆ k = Σ_{i=1}^{L} q^(i)T R(∆θ_i) k^(i)."

### Lemma 2 (Appendix A.2; "Per-block accounting of attention-logit error")
> "For a query at position n, a cached key at position m, and the equivalent pre-RoPE decoded key k̂_m = R_m^T k̂_m^R, let e_{k,m}^(i) = k_m^(i) − k̂_m^(i) and E_{n,m} := |K_{m−n}(q_n,k_m) − K_{m−n}(q_n,k̂_m)|. Then E_{n,m} ≤ Σ_i ‖q_n^(i)‖_2 ‖e_{k,m}^(i)‖_2."
Proof = triangle inequality + Cauchy–Schwarz + "Each R(∆θ_i) is a rotation, so it preserves the block norm". Assumptions: none beyond RoPE being block-orthogonal. Sec. 2.1 adds: "Each block thus contributes independently to the bound, with no cross-block terms."

### Coordinate change (Appendix A.1, Eq. 2)
> "Although the cache stores post-RoPE keys, the analysis can be written in pre-RoPE coordinates. If k̂_m^R is the decoded post-RoPE key and R_t denotes the absolute RoPE rotation at position t, define k̂_m := R_m^T k̂_m^R. Then, for a query at position n, (q_n^R)^T k̂_m^R = (R_n q_n)^T k̂_m^R = q_n^T R_n^T R_m k̂_m = q_n^T R_{m−n} k̂_m. (2) RoPE is orthogonal block by block, so this coordinate change does not change block norms."

### Theorem 1 (Sec. 3.2; proof Appendix A.4, exchange argument)
> "For positive scores s_i and feasible integer budget B ∈ [L b_min, L b_max], Algorithm 1 minimizes J(b) = Σ_i s_i 4^{−b_i} over all integer allocations satisfying b_min ≤ b_i ≤ b_max and Σ_i b_i = B."
Marginal gain used by the greedy: "∆_i(b_i) = s_i 4^{−b_i} − s_i 4^{−(b_i+1)} = (3/4) s_i 4^{−b_i}". Standard greedy-on-geometric-gains result; nothing model-specific.

## 2. What is HEURISTIC (the paper says so itself)

### Ideal block weight vs. surrogate (Sec. 2.3, 3.1, App. A.3)
> "In expectation, block i's contribution to the bound is the ideal block weight s*_i := E[‖q^(i)‖_2 ‖k^(i)‖_2] times the local quantizer's bit-dependent rate (Appendix A.3)."
> "Directly estimating s*_i requires paired query-key products, which can be noisy on a short calibration prefix. Block-GTQ instead uses an AM-GM-based energy score that depends only on marginal Q/K second moments: s_i := ½ E[‖q^(i)‖²_2 + ‖k^(i)‖²_2]. By AM-GM, s*_i ≤ s_i in expectation: s_i may overestimate s*_i but never underestimates it."
GQA-aware instantiation, Eq. (1)/(4): s_{ℓ,h,i} = ½ [ E_{t, g∈G(h)} ‖q^(i)_{ℓ,g,t}‖² + E_t ‖k^(i)_{ℓ,h,t}‖² ], Q-side averaged over the query heads reading KV head h ("Averaging the Q vectors over heads before squaring would yield a strictly smaller value by Jensen's inequality").

### The objective is admitted to be a proxy (App. A.3)
> "The resulting allocation objective J(b) = Σ_i s_i 4^{−b_i} should be read as a rate-allocation proxy rather than a tight consequence of the absolute-error bound above: the score comes from the RoPE-logit sensitivity, while the factor 4^{−b_i} comes from the local MSE-oriented quantizer."
> "Squaring the per-block bound and applying Cauchy–Schwarz gives E²_{n,m} ≤ L Σ_i ‖q_n^(i)‖²‖e^(i)_{k,m}‖²; together with the TQ-MSE squared-error bound E‖e^(i)‖² ≲ 4^{−b_i}‖k^(i)‖², this yields a mean-squared logit-error bound of the form Σ_i 4^{−b_i} E[‖q^(i)‖² ‖k^(i)‖²]."
The 4^{−b} factor is TQ-MSE's rate law (Sec. 2.2): "at b bits per coordinate, the decoded vector x̂ satisfies E‖x − x̂‖² ≤ ‖x‖² C_TQ 4^{−b}" (constant printed as 3π/2 with a displaced radical in the extraction, i.e. most likely √3·π/2).

### Allocation rule summary
Energy-based, SYMMETRIC in Q and K (arithmetic mean of query and key block second moments), label-free, computed per (layer, KV head) on a 2048-token WikiText-2 calibration prefix; greedy integer allocation under Σ b_i = B; blocks with the same bit width are concatenated and encoded by one TQ-MSE encoder (post-RoPE key blocks, Sec. 3.2). V uses uniform TQ-MSE. No block is dropped. Bit-width range used in experiments is 1–8 b (inferred from the Table 8 / Figure 3 columns 1b…8b; the text never states b_min/b_max numerically).

Score ablation (App. C.2, Table 15; NIAH Overall on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct at K2V2): qk_avg 97.36 (default), qk_max 96.77, k_only 95.29, qk_product (= sqrt(E‖q‖²·E‖k‖²), the geometric mean) 95.12, q_only 94.05. So query-side energy is already in the score; the product form closer to s*_i was tried and was slightly worse than the arithmetic mean.

## 3. Pre-RoPE vs post-RoPE: NOT analysed as a quantization choice
- Deployment quantizes POST-RoPE keys: "we concatenate the corresponding post-RoPE key blocks and encode the resulting subvector with one TQ-MSE encoder at b bits/dim" (Sec. 3.2); "Although deployment stores post-RoPE keys, we analyze E_{n,m} in pre-RoPE coordinates with relative offset ∆ = m − n" (Sec. 2.3).
- The only pre/post statement is invariance: "Since each RoPE block is an orthogonal 2 × 2 rotation, RoPE preserves the ℓ2 norm of every query/key block, so norm-based block statistics are RoPE-invariant—we can therefore compute the energy score in pre-RoPE coordinates." (Sec. 2.1).
- Related work only notes "KVQuant and RotateKV both operate before RoPE" (Sec. 5). There is NO experiment comparing pre-RoPE vs post-RoPE quantization, no numbers, no models for that question.
- Note for the report: TQ-MSE "applies a shared orthogonal rotation, scalar-quantizes the rotated coordinates, and restores the radius" (Sec. 2.2), i.e. its MSE bound depends only on ‖x‖, so under the report's own Lemma 9.1 the pre/post choice is immaterial for this quantizer. Block-GTQ is therefore consistent with, but gives no evidence for, the report's lemma.
- Also: the paper never uses the words low-/high-frequency; it makes no claim about WHICH frequencies receive more bits. Allocation is by block energy, which is RoPE-invariant, not by frequency index.

## 5. Limitations / future work (paper's own words, Sec. 7)
> "Limitations and future work. Block-GTQ allocates bits only on K, leaving V uniform. A V-side allocator, joint K+V optimization, and denser packing could further reduce memory. The fused decode path is an initial single-GPU implementation; multi-GPU and batched serving are open directions."
Additional caveats stated elsewhere: the objective is "a rate-allocation proxy" (A.3); the energy score is an AM-GM upper bound that "may overestimate s*_i" (3.1); MLA latent is "outside its scope" (B.1); no-buffer drop on DS-R1-Llama-8B (6.3.2).

## 6. Implications for the report (아이디어 9)
1. Line 322 ("2-D 주파수 블록별 에너지 비트 할당") is accurate but should say the score is the symmetric Q+K block energy ½E[‖q^(i)‖²+‖k^(i)‖²] (an AM-GM upper bound of the ideal weight E[‖q^(i)‖‖k^(i)‖]), with greedy integer allocation on Σ s_i 4^{−b_i} (Theorem 1), on top of TQ-MSE.
2. Line 331 (따름정리 9.3: "Block-GTQ의 key 에너지 할당에 query 에너지 가중(OSCAR/Spend-Bits의 논점)을 결합한 형태") is WRONG as stated: Block-GTQ already includes query energy. The report's reverse water-filling b_f = ½log₂(|q_f|²σ_f²/θ) is the continuous relaxation of exactly Block-GTQ's objective with the product weight |q_f|²σ_f² in place of the paper's arithmetic-mean weight; the paper's Table 15 already ablates the geometric-mean product form (95.12 vs 97.36 NIAH at K2V2) and finds it slightly worse. The novelty of 9.3 must be reframed (e.g. as a Gaussian/MSE justification and a per-pair Fisher/softmax-weighted extension), not as "adding query weighting".
3. Line 318 ("이 보조정리는 … Block-GTQ의 주파수 블록 할당을 예측한다"): Block-GTQ's allocation is by RoPE-invariant block energy and the paper makes no low-/high-frequency claim, so the pair-mixing lemma does not "predict" it. What the lemma can say is that Block-GTQ's quantizer (TQ-MSE: shared orthogonal rotation + scalar quantization) is rotation-equivariant in the sense of 9.1, which is consistent with the paper quantizing post-RoPE keys while scoring in pre-RoPE coordinates.
4. Line 322 gap claim: the paper does NOT analyse pre- vs post-RoPE quantization; it only notes norm invariance. The gap statement "양자화기 의존성을 설명한 논문이 없다" survives, and Block-GTQ can be cited as a post-RoPE, rotation-based quantizer that sidesteps the question.
5. Tag should move from [S] to [V] (full text verified). Experiment (d) "9.3 할당 vs 균일 할당" should instead compare against Block-GTQ's qk_avg allocation (already beats uniform by 32–80% MAE on 10 models), otherwise it duplicates the paper.
