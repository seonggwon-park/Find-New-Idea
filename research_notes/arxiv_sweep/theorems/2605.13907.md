## Q1. "KL(quantized || FP) grows monotonically as the policy sharpens" — status: EMPIRICAL OBSERVATION, no theorem

Exact statement (Section 3, right after Eq. 6):

> "The induced bias scales with D_KL(π_θ̂old ∥ π_θold), which we observe grows monotonically during training as the policy sharpens and quantization artifacts interact with increasingly peaked distributions. This compounding effect degrades both sample efficiency and final performance [Yao et al., 2025a]."

- Direction: KL(quantized rollout policy π_θ̂old ∥ full-precision learner π_θold). Figure 4 labels the curve `|KL(π_roll ∥ π_train)|` (absolute value, i.e. a plug-in estimate).
- "we observe": there is no theorem, lemma, or derivation about monotonicity or about "sharpening". The word "entropy" does not occur anywhere in the paper; "sharpening" is never measured (no entropy, no logit-norm, no max-prob curve). The causal link "sharpens → KL grows" is asserted, not tested.
- Evidence = one model, one task: **Figure 4 (Section 3.1), LLaDA-8B, GSM8K, ~0–30k steps**, three panels for FP8 rollout (no correction) / TIS / AIS: (i) |KL(π_roll ∥ π_train)| (y-axis ~0.00–0.05), (ii) E[|Δp|] (~0.005–0.0175), (iii) ESS ratio (0.65–0.95). Text:
  > "Across all three metrics, the uncorrected FP8 rollout steadily drifts away from the learner as distributional sharpening intensifies, with its ESS ratio decaying from above 0.95 to below 0.70. TIS partially mitigates this drift but still trails AIS on all three panels"
- Supporting curves: Figure 3 (LLaDA-8B, GSM8K, 0–40k steps) — CV = σ(w)/μ(w) of the importance weights; "C=10 lets CV grow rapidly, C=2 over-suppresses the corrective signal, and C=5 sits between the two. As the policy sharpens, no single C remains right." Figure 6 (Appendix E.1, GSM8K, ×10⁻³ "train–rollout mismatch", 0–20k steps, FP8Rollout / TIS c=20 / TIS c=5 / AIS).
- **No KL/ESS/mismatch trajectory is shown for Qwen3-8B or Qwen3.5-9B.** For Qwen the only dynamics figure is Figure 1 (Qwen3-8B, GSM8K reward trajectory) annotated "Early training — noise as exploration bonus / Late training — noise as destabilizing bias"; this is an illustration of the thesis, not a KL measurement.
- The "double-edged sword" (exploration early, bias late) is explicitly a hypothesis:
  - Sec. 4.2: "We attribute occasional gains beyond BF16 to stochastic FP8 perturbations acting as exploration noise, leaving a precise characterization to future work."
  - App. E.1: "We hypothesize that the stochastic perturbations introduced by FP8 quantization act as an implicit exploration bonus, driving the policy into low-probability regions of the trajectory space. … A formal characterization is left to future work."
  - App. F: "our exploration-bonus claim is supported empirically rather than by a formal causal analysis".
- The paper never says the KL is non-monotone. It says monotone increase. The non-stationarity it emphasises is in the *effect* (benign → harmful), not in the *shape* of the KL curve.

## Q2. The adaptive IS scheme — token entropy is NOT used

Section 3.1, Eqs. 9–18; Section 3.2 Eq. 19 + Algorithm 1; Appendix D.

Mixed estimator (Eq. 9–10):
> ĝ = E_{x∼π_rollout}[(1 − α(x) + α(x) w̄(x)) A(x) ∇θ log π_train(x)],   w(x) = π_train(x)/π_rollout(x),  w̄(x) = min(w(x), C),  α(x) ∈ [0,1]

Oracle coefficient (Eq. 11; App. D Prop. 1, Eq. 25–26): surrogate MSE (1−α)²‖b0‖² + α²σ1² gives
> α* = ‖b0‖² / (‖b0‖² + σ1²)

Practical α (Eq. 17) is the product of a "reliability gate" and a "necessity gate", computed from **mini-batch scalar reductions**:
- Eq. 12 (weight reliability): α_ess = sqrt(ESS_ratio) = (1 + CV²(w̄))^(−1/2), CV over the current mini-batch.
- Eq. 13–14 (divergence severity): d̄ = (1/|B|) Σ_{x_t∈B} |log π_train(x_t|x_<t) − log π_rollout(x_t|x_<t)| over all token positions in the mini-batch; α_mis = min(1, d̄/δ).
- Eq. 15–16 (variance amplification): Δσ = std(A(x)·w̄(x)) / (std(A(x)) + ε); α_var = max(0, (Δσ − γ)/γ).
- Eq. 17: α = clip(α_ess − β·α_var, 0, 1) · α_mis.
- Eq. 18: Ã(x) = [1 + α·(w̄(x) − 1)] A(x).
- GRPO instantiation (Eq. 19, Alg. 1): per-token rollout–training ratio ρ^t_rt,i = exp(log π_train − log π_rollout) at θ_old; rectification weight w̃^t_i = 1 + α(min(ρ^t_rt,i, C) − 1); multiplied into the standard clipped PPO/GRPO surrogate; the old–new ratio ρ^t_on,i is kept separate.
- Hyperparameters (App. C.4): C = 5, δ = 0.02, γ = 1.2, ε = 1e−6, "Tuned on a GSM8K validation split and held fixed across all LLaDA and Qwen tasks." Signals computed on detached tensors.
- Abstract: "adjusts the strength of its intervention on a per-batch basis"; Sec. 3.1: "Treating α as a scalar for analytical tractability". So in practice α is one scalar per mini-batch (Eq. 9 writes α(x) but all three signals are batch statistics).

Token entropy: **not used, not mentioned.** `grep -i entrop` on the full text returns nothing. There is no per-token or per-position gating; the only per-token quantity is the clipped ratio min(ρ_t, C), exactly as in FlashRL/TIS. AIS = TIS with a batch-level interpolation knob between "no correction" and "TIS correction". The paper does not discuss where along the sequence the mismatch concentrates (no fork/high-entropy token analysis).

## What is PROVED (Appendix D) vs. CLAIMED

Assumptions (D.1): (A1) |A(x)| ≤ M_A; (A2) ‖s(x)‖ = ‖∇θ log π_train(x)‖ ≤ M_s; (A3) supp(π_train) ⊆ supp(π_rollout); (A4) negligible truncation bias ‖b1‖ ≪ ‖b0‖ (set b1 = 0).

- Prop. 1 (Oracle mixing coefficient), under (A1)–(A4): α*_exact = (‖b0‖² + σ0² − κ)/(‖b0‖² + σ0² + σ1² − 2κ) (Eq. 25); reduces to Eq. 26 α* = ‖b0‖²/(‖b0‖²+σ1²) when σ1² ≫ σ0² and σ1² ≫ |κ| ("an approximation supported by the empirical observation"). Note: α is treated as a constant scalar; the proof does not cover a data-dependent α(x).
- Prop. 2 (Bounded second moment), under (A1)–(A3): E_rollout‖ĝ(α)‖² ≤ (1 + α(C−1))² M_A² M_s². Trivial from 0 ≤ 1−α+αw̄ ≤ 1+α(C−1).
- Prop. 3 (On-policy recovery): if π_rollout = π_train a.e. then w = w̄ = 1 and ĝ = g for any α(x).
- Nothing is proved about bias of the practical α, about monotonicity of KL, about sharpening, or about entropy.

## Paper's own Limitations (Appendix F, verbatim)

> "We acknowledge several limitations. First, our experiments focus on 8–9B-parameter models in the BF16-trainer/FP8-rollout setup, and scaling to larger models or more aggressive quantization (e.g., FP4, INT4) remains an open question that may require re-tuning C, δ, γ. Second, we evaluate on mathematical reasoning and planning, leaving instruction-following, dialogue, code generation, and RLHF-style preference learning unexplored. Third, the oracle coefficient in Proposition 1 relies on simplifying assumptions (negligible truncation bias and dominant clipped IS variance) that may not hold under heavier quantization or smaller batches. Fourth, our exploration-bonus claim is supported empirically rather than by a formal causal analysis, and isolating exploration from other mechanisms (gradient regularization, implicit smoothing) is left to future work. Finally, compute constraints limit each configuration to a single seed, and a study of run-to-run variance would further strengthen the empirical claims."

Also Sec. 4.2 / App. E.1: "A formal characterization [of quantization noise as exploration] is left to future work."

## Implications for the report (`reports/LLM 양자화 연구 아이디어 10선.md`)

1. Line 234: the AIS gloss "KL이 정책 날카로워짐과 함께 단조 증가, 초기엔 탐색 도움 후기엔 편향" is faithful to the paper's wording, but tag it as: (a) an empirical observation stated in Sec. 3 with no theorem; (b) KL/ESS curves shown only for LLaDA-8B on GSM8K (Fig. 4; Fig. 3, Fig. 6), not for Qwen; (c) the "exploration early / bias late" part is the authors' hypothesis (Sec. 4.2, App. E.1, App. F). Change [S] → [V].
2. Line 242: "두 효과의 곱이 KL의 비단조 궤적(AIS)을 만든다" is WRONG as an attribution — AIS reports a *monotonically increasing* KL, not a non-monotone one. Rewrite: the report's δ ∝ ‖z‖ × (1 − ‖p‖²) model predicts a possibly non-monotone KL; AIS's Fig. 4 shows only monotone growth over ~30k steps on LLaDA-8B/GSM8K, so a non-monotone trajectory would be a *new, falsifiable prediction that goes beyond AIS*, not an AIS observation.
3. Line 230 (iii): "δ가 커져 KL이 다시 증가한다는 AIS의 관찰" — drop "다시" (AIS never reports a prior decrease) and drop the logit-scale mechanism attribution (AIS gives no formula; it only says "quantization artifacts interact with increasingly peaked distributions").
4. The gap claim stands and can be sharpened: AIS uses no token entropy and no per-position gating; its adaptivity is a single per-batch scalar α built from ESS of clipped weights, mean |Δ log p| over all tokens, and an advantage-variance ratio, on top of a fixed per-token clip C = 5. The report's entropy-selective (fork-token) IS is therefore orthogonal to AIS and could be combined with it. AIS does report KL, E|Δp|, ESS, CV and reward curves, but never entropy — consistent with "엔트로피, KL(μ‖π), 보상을 한 런에서 함께 보고한 논문이 없다".
5. Do not cite AIS as evidence about FP4/INT4 or NVFP4: it is FP8 E4M3 only and lists FP4/INT4 as open (App. F). Any FP4 statement in the report attributed to AIS must be removed.
6. Treat AIS > BF16 gains (e.g., +6.63 AIME25 Qwen3-8B, +9.50 Countdown-128 LLaDA) with caution: single seed, best-of-checkpoints selection, and the paper itself calls the exploration mechanism a hypothesis.
