## Bottom line (what the citation graph adds)

1. **The proof skeleton of "정리 2" is already published in an ML-venue preprint.** Soltanalian, *When Can Depth Replace Precision? A Resource Theory of Quantized Neural Computation* (2607.23390, 2026-07-25), Sec. 4.3 "Increment error feedback", eqs. (40)–(42): q_k = Q(h G(ẑ_k) + r_k), ẑ_{k+1} = ẑ_k + q_k, r_{k+1} = h G(ẑ_k) + r_k − q_k; "Assume the quantizer has residual radius at most ρ on the entire declared carry range, the carry register does not overflow, and hence ‖r_k‖ ≤ ρ throughout execution." **Theorem 8** bounds max_k ‖ẑ_k − z_k‖ ≤ ρ e^{L_z T} + η_G Φ(T); the proof defines "the transformed error s_k = e_k + r_k", gets s_{k+1} = s_k + h{G(ẑ_k) − G(z_k)}, and closes with "‖e_k‖ ≤ ‖s_k‖ + ‖r_k‖ ≤ ρ(1+hL_z)^k ≤ ρ e^{L_z T}". That is the report's g_t := d_t + r_t device verbatim. The same paper states the deadband in one line: "In the scalar recursion x_{k+1} = Q_Δ(x_k + D^{-1}), x_0 = 0, every update rounds away whenever D > 2/Δ, so the implemented state remains zero although the ideal endpoint is one", and in the abstract "full-state write-back introduces a Dρ_z penalty and can freeze residual updates, whereas increment error feedback replaces this growth by a bounded carry term." What it does **not** have: a contraction/decay factor a_t ∈ [0,1) (its bound is expansive, Gronwall e^{L T}; Remark 49 treats ẋ = a − λx only for the endpoint error without feedback), any state cache / SSM / LLM, the b_r-bit residual analysis, or bit allocation. It also says explicitly: "The present contribution is not the invention of feedback quantization." → the report's novelty must be stated as *the decay-uniform (1 − ∏ a_s) telescoping bound for time-varying gated recurrences plus its residual-storage corollary*, not "error feedback bounds the state error".

2. **The classical source of the bound is first-order ΣΔ stability.** Daubechies & Saab (1506.00179, 2015) restate it: "One-bit, first order, greedy ΣΔ quantization produces bits q_n [by the] recursion, with initial condition u_0, |u_0| < 1 … One can see, by induction, that |u_n| < 1 for all n", and attribute the stable-schemes family to Daubechies & DeVore (Annals 2003). 2607.23390 §2.3 cites the same lineage (Daubechies–DeVore 2003; Güntürk 2003; Krahmer et al. 2012) and O'Connor & Welling's *Sigma Delta Quantized Networks* (1611.02024) as the neural precedent. In the leaky-integrator (a < 1) case the report's |d_t| ≤ Δ is the L1-norm of the error-transfer impulse response (1 − z⁻¹)/(1 − a z⁻¹), which equals 2 for every a ∈ [0,1): a textbook error-spectrum-shaping fact (Higgins & Munson, "error spectrum shaping versus the optimal linear state-space formulation", cited by Zheng 2412.05651/2506.01404). The honest framing: "we transplant a classical ΣΔ/ESS bound to time-varying selective recurrences and quantify its storage cost".

3. **Q-Mamba (ACL 2025 Findings) is the strongest existence-preemption and already writes down the error recursion, then drops it.** Eq. (9a) h^q_t = Ā Q(h^q_{t−1}) + B̄ x_t ("we store state caches as low-bit elements, then load and dequantize them before computation at the next timestep"); eq. (11) δ_t = Ā_t (Q(h_{t−1} + δ_{t−1}) − h_{t−1}) — "the quantization error propagated from the previous timestep, δ_{t−1}, and the quantization error introduced in the current timestep" — followed by "Assuming that quantization errors δ_{t−1} are sufficiently small compared to the hidden state h_{t−1}, we discard δ_{t−1}". The report's whole point is that this term is *not* small for a ≈ 1. Table 1 (WikiText2 PPL, Mamba2 130M/370M/780M/1.3B/2.7B): FP16 20.04/14.16/11.81/10.42/9.06; **W16A16H4 baseline (naive per-step 4-bit state) 976.56/913.34/865.78/1556.15/116.23 → Q-Mamba 45.73/22.24/19.07/15.20/11.55**; W16A16H6 baseline 249.09/134.91/38.04/23.62/13.60 → 23.79/15.33/12.69/11.37/9.59; W16A16H8 baseline 20.97/14.83/12.04/10.52/9.11 → 20.49/14.26/11.86/10.51/9.11; W8A8H4 2.7B baseline 364.84 → 12.99. Table 2 (zero-shot avg, W8A8H4, 2.7B): FP 58.63, baseline 55.30, Q-Mamba 56.52. Fix = Decoupled Scale Quantization (state-dim × channel-dim scales using sqrt(mean) norms; abs.max → inf PPL, Table 7) + Efficient Selectivity Reconstruction (fine-tune B, C, Δ, norm, conv, ≈2% of params, 128 samples, 3 epochs). No EF/SR/dither, no time-growth analysis. App. A.5: FP Mamba2-2.7B "suffers from significant performance degradation at a sequence length of 4K" (Stuffed Mamba) while "the quantized model maintains stable performance even at 8K" — quantization suppresses dominant outlier channels. Not on arXiv (aclanthology 2025.findings-acl.551; PDF downloaded).

4. **A published negative result uses the exact words "ruling out sigma-delta noise-shaping for SSMs".** Ternary Mamba 2606.18114 §6.3: "We additionally tested sigma-delta (ΣΔ) modulation—successful in 1-bit DACs for temporally correlated signals. The method requires within-group smoothness (TV(x) < L1(x)). Measuring the TV/L1 ratio across all 96 projection inputs in Mamba-2: i.i.d. Gaussian (theoretical) 1.414, Mamba grand mean (96 hooks) 1.421 … ΣΔ is inapplicable." Fig. 5 caption: "TV/L1 ratio exceeds the Gaussian baseline (√2) at every layer, ruling out sigma-delta noise-shaping for SSMs." This is ΣΔ *along the feature dimension of projection inputs* (spatial, weight-quantization-side), not EF on the recurrent state over time; the state sequence h_t with a ≈ 1 is maximally autocorrelated in t, so the TV/L1 argument does not transfer. The report must cite and rebut this explicitly or reviewers will.

5. **Citation hubs show the two literatures do not touch.** DAMP 2608.27513, STEPQuant 2609.38169, LeapQuant 2609.38166 and 2609.30950 cite Quamba/Quamba2/Q-Mamba/MambaQuant/KIVI/KVQuant/TurboQuant/SGLang/vLLM and nothing from the ΣΔ/dither/ESS literature (grep for "error feedback|noise shap|dither|sigma" = 0 hits in all four). 2609.39185 is the only one citing Schreier–Temes, Gray–Stockham, Schuchman, Forsythe 1959, Connolly–Higham–Mary and Croci et al., and it declines EF for the storage reason. Conversely 2607.23390 cites Daubechies–DeVore, O'Connor–Welling, SDQ-LLM, ECO and no SSM paper. The bridge is open; the report's theorem is the bridge.

6. **Systems baselines from the neighbourhood that reduce the number of re-quantizations** (and therefore the accumulation the idea attacks): Nemotron 3 Ultra 2606.15007 §4.5 "periodic cache checkpointing: Instead of storing (thus, quantizing) the state in each decoding step, we store it every C steps … we cache the input activations and apply activation replay"; shipped config "FP16 SSM cache storage with stochastic rounding … mitigating the recurrent rounding bias observed with naive FP16 round-to-nearest cache storage"; "FP8 E4M3 quantization degrades accuracy, likely because it provides lower effective precision for these cache values than INT8". ReplaySSM (Liou & Dao, blog June 2026, cited by LeapQuant): "Cache SSM inputs, not state", write back only when the input buffer fills, up to 1.43× decode in vLLM. LeapQuant: per-16-token-window quantization. SGLang flags (per web search of the Nemotron3-Ultra cookbook): `--mamba-ssm-dtype float16`, `--enable-mamba-cache-stochastic-rounding` (requires FP16 + CUDA; SM100 with the triton backend), `--mamba-cache-philox-rounds`. vLLM `--mamba-ssm-cache-dtype`. Nobody ships INT8/FP8 state or EF.

7. **vLLM #55196 refreshed (2026-10-10):** still open, "No branches or pull requests"; only post-09-20 comment is the author (09-25) asking tomalmog ("Thanks for the implementation, can you please test your changes and create PR?") and zz774ever to open PRs. tomalmog's 09-15 INT8+EF / INT8+int4-residual numbers link a repository, not a PR. Nothing new.

8. **Delta-rule hypothesis gets architectural support.** GDN-2 (2605.22791, "Decoupling Erase and Write in Linear Attention") App. D: "The recurrent state is stored in fp32 across chunks and during recurrent decoding"; "the local cumulative sum G_r = Σ_{i≤r} g_i is a path-length-dependent quantity. A low precision mantissa can perturb long products" — i.e., the architects treat state and log-decay precision as first-order concerns, and the erase/write decomposition is exactly the term the keyword note said must enter the model for GDN/KDA.

## Consequences for Idea 2

- Rewrite the novelty claim: not "ΣΔ bounds recurrent-state error" (2607.23390 Thm 8, Daubechies–DeVore, ESS) but "for time-varying a_t ∈ [0,1) the first-order EF error is ≤ Δ uniformly in t and a (via 1 − ∏a_s), unlike RTN Δ/(2(1−a)) and SR Δ√(t/12); with b_r-bit residual storage the worst case is Δ2^{−(b_r+1)}/(1−a) and the stochastic sd Δ2^{−b_r}/√(24(1−a))" — and position against Weyl dither (zero storage) at equal total bits.
- Add Q-Mamba (W8A8H4, learned scales + ESR) and DAMP/STEPQuant/2609.30950 as baselines; add checkpointing (Nemotron Ultra C=8), per-window (LeapQuant), ReplaySSM-style deferred write-back as "fewer quantization events" baselines.
- Rebut Ternary Mamba §6.3 in one paragraph (TV/L1 along features vs along time).
- Fold 2609.30950's admission (deadband correlations break the Gaussian model) into the ΣΔ + Gramian-allocation story; cite Huang & Schultheiss 1963 for water-filling.
- Keep the Mamba-vs-delta-rule split hypothesis; GDN-2's erase/write decomposition gives the exact algebra.
