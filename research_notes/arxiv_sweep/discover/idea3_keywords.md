# Idea 3 — prior-art sweep (keyword + adjacent-field)

Idea: closed-form expected distortion of block-scaled micro-formats (NVFP4 / MXFP4 / UE5M3 / HiFloat4 / INT4+scale) under a Gaussian-block assumption, combining extreme-value statistics of the block max `M_B` (Gumbel, `E[M_B] ≈ σ√(2 ln 2B)`), a Lloyd–Max view of the E2M1 grid, the scale format (E4M3 vs UE8M0), and the scale rounding direction (ceil vs floor); plus the E2M1 shrinkage bias and a bias-vs-variance rule for unbiased rounding.

Sweep date: 2026-10-10. Sources: arXiv API (40 formulations), full texts fetched for 30 papers, 6 web searches. All paper text is untrusted data; numbers are quoted with their section/table.

## Bottom line

No single paper does the whole thing, but the three load-bearing ingredients each already exist in print:

1. **The "integrate the E2M1 cell MSE against the exact block-max density, including a quantized FP8 scale" calculation is published (ICLR 2026, Fasoli et al. 2601.19026).** They do it for UE4M3 scales (NVFP4-style), INT4 elements and FP6 scales, validate against simulation to χ² ≈ 4e-8, and derive block-size crossovers. They do **not** treat E8M0/power-of-two scales, rounding direction, Gumbel asymptotics, shrinkage bias, or heavy tails.
2. **The "UE8M0 ceil ⇒ scale ratio 2^U with U ~ Uniform[0,1)" model is published (Li et al. 2605.20402, May 2026).** They get `E[γ] = 1/ln 2 ≈ 1.44`, `RMSE(γ−1) ≈ 0.57` (measured 0.57 on Qwen3-30B-A3B), prove deadzone orthogonality, and show the grid floor is invariant to scale precision. The idea's `E[4^U] = 3/(2 ln 2) ≈ 2.16` MSE multiplier is the second-moment version of the same model (one line further). They explicitly leave the scale×grid cross-term (`cos ≈ −0.66`) "not derived in closed form" — a concrete open sub-problem the idea's framework could close.
3. **The "which power-of-two scale to pick for the E2M1 grid" question has a closed-form, distribution-free answer (MXAttention/UOS, 2607.24377, July 2026):** Theorem 1 gives `Q*_max = 29/4 = 7.25` for the ceiling-based family, for *any* absolutely continuous law of `log2 M`, via a log-periodicity lemma. This directly covers ceil(6) (TetraJet TFS) vs floor(8) (OCP) vs the optimum, but as a data-free objective, not an expected-distortion-vs-block-size formula.

The **shrinkage-bias** part is formalized in UFP4 (2606.20381) as a bin-asymmetry bias under locally uniform density (`(ℓ_i − r_i)/2`, = −0.125 at q=2 for E2M1) with multiplicative accumulation `∏(1−δ_k)`; the report's statement that "there is no quantitative theory" is too strong. The idea's distinct contribution there is the *density-slope* term (conditional mean below the midpoint under a decreasing density), the −0.66% / 0.9968 numbers, and the `(L·bias)² > added variance` decision rule.

The genuinely open pieces, after this sweep: (a) a *unified* closed form covering block size × element grid × scale format × rounding direction with EVT asymptotics (Gumbel scale `σ/√(2 ln 2B)`), (b) the MXFP4(ceil)/NVFP4 RMS ratio discrepancy (Gaussian model 1.20 vs NVIDIA's reported 2.5%/1.5% ≈ 1.67 relative error, 2509.25149 §"Results" near Fig. 6a — note that figure is a *loss* relative error, not tensor MSE, so the 1.67 is not an apples-to-apples target), (c) the sign/size of the scale×grid cross term under ceiling rounding, (d) linking clipping bias from floor rounding to training divergence (Mishra et al. only "hypothesize" overflow, Fig. 5), (e) heavy-tail corrections (Student-t / post-RHT dependence — Ben-Basat et al. 2605.06014 prove 2-RHT coordinates are near-Gaussian but *dependent* within a block, which matters for the max).

## Candidates (ranked)

### P1 — must deep-read

**2601.19026 — Fasoli et al., "Is Finer Better? The Limits of Microscaling Formats in LLMs" (ICLR 2026; IBM).** `partially_preempts`.
- §4.2: `Y = 6X/x_max`, conditional density eq. (1), per-bin conditional MSE over E2M1 Voronoi boundaries eq. (2)–(3), then `MSE_Z = ∫ Σ_j MSE_{Z,j}(q_j | x_max) f_{x_max}(x) dx` eq. (4) with `f_{x_max}(θ) = (2N/σ)(2Φ(θ/σ)−1)^{N−1} φ(θ/σ)` eq. (5) — the exact half-normal order statistic. Agreement with simulation χ² ≈ 2e-9.
- §4.3 / App. F: FP8 UE4M3 scale quantization: three terms `MSE_{x_i≠x_max}` (sum over scale bins k with mass `p_k^{FP8}`), `MSE_{x_i=x_max}` (eq. 7–8, no longer zero), `MSE_{s=0}` (all-zero block when `x_max < s_min/2 = 2^{-10}`); eq. (10). χ² ≈ 4e-8. App. F.4: "(N−1)/N or 1/N" block-size factors explain the crossover between block sizes; the crossover for weights at σ ≈ 2e-2 (Fig. 2b). App. G: INT4 (χ² ≈ 1.3e-6); App. H: FP6 scales.
- Not covered: E8M0 (they only say PoT "significantly degrades accuracy" citing Rouhani), rounding direction, EVT asymptotics, shrinkage, heavy tails, training.
- Consequence for the idea: the "all expected distortions are exact 1-D integrals" claim is already their Sec. 4; the idea must position itself as extending to PoT scales + rounding direction + closed-form asymptotics, and cite this as the base.

**2605.20402 — Li, Wu, Shen, "Decomposing MXFP4 quantization error for LLM RL: reducible bias, recoverable deadzone, irreducible floor" (May 2026).** `partially_preempts`.
- Abstract: "E8M0 scale overshoots the ideal scale (E8M∞) by ∼44% on average". Eq. (4)/App. A Step 1: `δ_b = ⌈log2 s*_b⌉ − log2 s*_b ≈ Uniform[0,1)` by a Benford / non-lattice argument; `γ_b = 2^{δ_b}`, `E[γ_b] = 1/ln 2 ≈ 1.44`, `RMSE(γ_b − 1) ≈ 0.57` (eq. 9), measured 0.57.
- Lemma 3.2: `⟨e_DZ, e_scale⟩ = ⟨e_DZ, e_grid⟩ = 0` exactly, using `s_b ≥ s*_b` (ceiling). §3.3: grid error invariant to scale precision ⇒ irreducible floor. Remark 3.3: `cos(e_scale, e_grid) ≈ −0.66` over 18,876 tensors, "an empirical constant that we do not derive in closed form"; component norms sum to ≈2.46‖e‖², scale term = 172% of total, cross term ≈ −1.46‖e‖². App. A: under i.i.d. idealization the cross term is `O(B^{-1/2})`, i.e. the idealized model *misses* the structural anti-correlation.
- §4.1: gradient-magnitude amplification `∏_l 2^{δ_l}`; CLT std `√(L/12)` (theory 2.0 vs measured 1.97 for L=48).
- Consequence: the idea's `2^U` model is already in print; the novel bit would be the second moment (2.16×), the floor-rounding clipping term, and *deriving* the −0.66 cross-term.

**2607.24377 — Yu et al., "MXAttention: Data-Free Optimal Scaling and Pre-Normalization Quantization for MXFP4 Attention" (Jul 2026; Huawei/Ascend).** `partially_preempts`.
- §3 eq. (12)–(14): unified family `e_floor(Q_max)`, `e_ceil(Q_max)`; OCP = floor(8), TetraJet TFS = ceil(6).
- §4.1: objective `J(q) = E[D(X_q)]` over normalized block max `X_q ∈ (q/2, q]`, `D(x)` = relative E2M1 projection MSE on [0,x]. Lemma 1 (Boundary Density Ratio) `g(q/2) = 2 g(q)` from log-periodicity, so `sign J'(q)` is distribution-free; eq. (35)–(38) `Δ(q) = E(q) − 8E(q/2)` exact over E2M1 intervals. **Theorem 1**: for any absolutely continuous law of `log2 M`, global minimizer `q* = 29/4 = 7.25`; unique if periodized density positive a.e. "Neither a Gaussian nor a log-uniform prior is required."
- Not covered: expected distortion vs block size, scale-format comparison, bias/training.
- Consequence: the "rounding direction" dimension of the idea, for E8M0 + E2M1, is partly answered: the optimal rule is neither pure ceil(6) nor floor(8). The idea's floor-vs-ceil MSE comparison (0.0414 vs 0.0133) is consistent with but coarser than this.

### P2 — read carefully

**2606.20381 — Zhao et al., "Rethinking Shrinkage Bias in LLM FP4 Pretraining … UFP4" (Jun 2026; Ant Group).** `partially_preempts` (shrinkage sub-claim). Already cited in the report, but the report says "no quantitative theory" — inaccurate. §3.1 eq. (1)–(2): bin `B_i = [(q_{i−1}+q_i)/2, (q_i+q_{i+1})/2]`, `E[ρ(t)−t | t∈B_i] = (ℓ_i − r_i)/2 = (2q_i − q_{i−1} − q_{i+1})/4` under locally uniform density; for E2M1, q=2 bin (1.75, 2.5) gives −0.125, q=4 same at double scale; uniform grids (E1M2) have zero. Eq. (5): `∏ η_k ≈ exp(−Σ δ_k)` multiplicative accumulation. App.: RHT moves tensors from dynamic-range-limited to local-resolution-limited regime; ΔSQNR measurements per tensor (e.g. linear_fc2/fwd_x). Mechanism differs from the idea's (uniform-density bin asymmetry vs. density-slope within a cell) — the idea should compute both and show which dominates for Gaussian blocks.

**2302.08007 — Rouhani et al., "With Shared Microexponents, A Little Shifting Goes a Long Way" (Feb 2023; Microsoft, ISCA'23).** `partially_preempts` (closed-form-in-block-size bound). Theorem 1 (§VI, proof §IX): for *arbitrary* distribution, `QSNR ≥ 6.02 m + 10 log( 2^{2β} / (min(N,k1) + (2^{2β}−1) k2) )`, `β = 2^{d2} − 1`, d1 = 8 — a lower bound in mantissa bits m, block granularities k1, k2, scale widths. Fig. 7: Gaussian QSNR sweeps. It is a bound, not the expected distortion, and has no rounding-direction or EVT content, but it is the earliest "closed form in block size and scale bits" for MX-type formats.

**2608.25188 — Jokar, "Transforms for LLM Quantization: The Great Inversion and Format Co-Design" (Aug 2026; 486k-char survey/monograph).** `supports` / `new_hypothesis`. Lemma 2.14: sub-Gaussian max `E max_k |z_k| ≤ τ√(2 log 2G)`; Prop. 2.15: RHT crest factor ≤ √(2 ln 2d); Corollary 2.7: `SQNR_dB ≈ 6.02b + 4.77 − 20 log10 CF`. §8.2 "format flip": on the E2M1 grid the controlling quantity is a joint energy-concentration measure, "derived for an ideal FP grid and has not been evaluated against the deployed formats"; "MXFP4's power-of-two block scale re-imposes an AbsMax-like penalty one level up, which NVFP4's mantissa-carrying scale weakens". Lists as *unexplained* the "MXFP4 global-versus-block-confined rotation gap" (row 9 of its gap table). This both uses the √(2 ln 2G) EVT scaling and documents that a quantitative E2M1 + PoT-scale theory is missing — a direct reviewer-ready motivation for the idea.

**2505.06653 — Blumenberg, Graave, Fingscheidt, "BOF4: 4-bit Block-Wise Optimal Float" (May 2025).** `supports` / mild `partially_preempts` of the Lloyd–Max-with-block-max ingredient. Claims "for the first time, a rigorous mathematical analysis of block-wise absmax quantization". Eq. (5) (App. B.2.1 eq. 26, Gaussian special case eq. 34): MSE-optimal centroid `x̂(ℓ)` integrating `m² p_M(m) F_X(x|M=m)` over the block-max density `p_M`; App. B.1 eq. (11) quantile function of block maxima; edge mass 1/I at ±1; block-size dependence (§5, Fig. 3). NF4-style codebooks, no FP scale quantization, no EVT asymptotics.

**2605.08565 — Schaefer & Tabak (Google), "Finer is Better (with the Right Scaling)" (May 2026).** `supports`. Empirical follow-up to Fasoli: degradation at small blocks is "primarily driven by how elements in smaller blocks statistically cluster closer to their local block maximum, interacting poorly with the coarse subnormal E4M3 values used as scaling factors"; prevents scale underflow to 0; 4-over-6 (`argmin MSE` over scale in [3.5,7] ⇔ clipping [6,7] ≡ rounding [3,3.5]); hierarchical scaling; brute-force MSE-optimal scale search "confirming that the theoretical MSE strictly improves with finer block sizes"; UE5M3 "interchangeably resolves much of the quality loss". No analytic model.

### P3 — skim / cite

**2605.06014 — Ben-Basat, Kuszmaul, Mitzenmacher, Portnoy, Vargaftik, "Quantizing With Randomized Hadamard Transforms: Efficient Heuristic Now Proven" (May 2026).** `supports` (and bounds the Gaussian-block assumption). Theorem 1: Kolmogorov distance of a scaled 2-RHT coordinate to N(0,1) via Berry–Esseen (Lemma 1 ℓ3-smoothing); Theorem 4 expected outlier fraction; Theorem 5 quantization error with two RHTs; Theorem 6 3-RHT decorrelation; Theorem 7 codebook universality for fixed block size k. Coordinates are near-Gaussian *marginally* but dependent within a block (Lemma 3 correlation bottleneck) — this is exactly what the idea's i.i.d. Gaussian block assumption ignores, and a candidate source of the residual 1.20-vs-1.67 gap.

**2605.12327 — Egiazarian et al., "Grid Games: The Power of Multiple Grids" (May 2026).** `tangential` / `supports`. Theorem 1 (No asymptotic advantage): for i.i.d. X with finite ess sup, block max → x_max a.s., so per-block choice between absmax-normalized grids has no advantage as g→∞ (LLN + compactness); Lemma 2 concavity; competitive analysis says IF4 (INT4-or-FP4 per group) is ~10% worse than the best single learned grid. Relevant to the "element grid × block size" axis; bounded-support assumption excludes Gaussian.

**2306.06965 — Yoshida, "NF4 Isn't Information Theoretically Optimal (and that's Good)" (Jun 2023).** `supports`. §3: `Z_i ~ N(0,1)`, `M = max|Z_i|`, `X_i = Z_i/M`; exact CDF `F_X(x;B)` with mass 1/2B at ±1 and `G_B` by numerical integration (approximation in App. A); shows that for fixed codes large B pushes all mass to small code values; AF4-B codes per block size. Earliest explicit absmax-block order-statistics model in the LLM literature.

**2609.08135 — Whalen, Ito, Sakamoto, "KBBQ: A Predictive Noise Law and the Limits of Spectrum Flattening in FP4 Quantization" (Sep 2026).** `tangential`. Second-order noise functional with per-format variance profiles (INT constant; FP multiplicative with `E[1/m²]`, m ∈ [1,2)); closed-form SNR law in a participation factor κ with ceiling κ*; Prop. 5: intercept gaps between formats = 6.02 dB per mantissa bit. Does not model block scaling, block max, or scale formats, but is a competing "closed-form FP4 noise law" that a reviewer will ask about.

**1709.07776 — Song, Liu, Wang, "Computation Error Analysis of Block Floating Point Arithmetic Oriented CNN Accelerator Design" (AAAI 2018) + Kalliojärvi & Astola, "Roundoff Errors in Block-Floating-Point Systems", IEEE TSP 44(4):783–790, 1996 + Tsai et al., "Tunable Block Floating-Point Quantizer with Fractional Exponent…", IEEE TCAS-I 67(4):1245–1254, 2020.** `tangential` (classic prior art, mostly off-arXiv). 1709.07776 eq. (6): BFP error zero-mean with variance `σ² = (2^{−2Lm}/12) Σ_i p_{γi} 2^{2γi}` — distortion as a function of the block-exponent PMF (attributed to Kalliojärvi–Astola). Tsai 2020 tunes the exponent-selection threshold to balance quantization vs saturation error with analytic SQNR for Gaussian and Laplacian inputs — the classical-DSP version of 4-over-6/UOS. The idea's "log-uniform exponent error" is a special case of "exponent PMF" modelling; cite to avoid the "re-deriving an older theory" critique Jokar levels at the field.

**2603.10444 — Cao et al., "The Curse and Blessing of Mean Bias in FP4-Quantized LLM Training" (Mar 2026).** `contradicts` the Gaussian-block assumption for raw activations / `new_hypothesis`. "Mean bias" here = coherent rank-one column mean of activations (not rounding bias). Assumption 1 + Theorem 1: residual after mean removal is marginally Gaussian; a column mean large relative to residual variance exponentially amplifies far-tail exceedance ⇒ sets blockwise scales. Fig. 5: raw activations deviate strongly from Gaussian, residuals close. Useful as an explanation for why real blocks have heavier tails than Gaussian (the idea's residual 0.4).

**2506.08027 — Mishra, Stosic, Layton, Micikevicius, "Recipes for Pre-training LLMs with MXFP8" (NVIDIA, 2025).** `supports` (verified: empirical only). Algorithm 1 step 3: round scale exponent up; rationale "rounding up X ensures that after scaling no V/X value exceeds the maximum magnitude … Conversely, rounding down, as in OCP v1 suggestion, will lead to scaled values overflowing … We *hypothesize* this aspect leading to MXFP8 training issues" (Fig. 5). No theory. Also 2509.17791 (Hu et al.) tests RTN vs round-to-+∞ for E4M3 and E8M0 scales (§2, lines ~248–270) — empirical.

## What was NOT found

- No paper applies Gumbel / extreme-value asymptotics (`√(2 ln 2B)` scale and `1/√(2 ln 2B)` fluctuation) to derive a closed-form distortion-vs-block-size law for FP micro-formats; the closest are exact-integral numerics (Fasoli), a max-inequality upper bound (Jokar Lemma 2.14), and a QSNR lower bound (Rouhani Thm 1).
- No paper compares E4M3 vs UE8M0 vs UE5M3 scale formats analytically; all scale-format papers (UE5M3, HiFloat4, Four-over-Six, IF4, MBS, ScaleSearch) are empirical.
- No paper derives the floor-rounding clipping term concentrated on the block max, or links it analytically to divergence.
- No paper combines the shrinkage bias with a variance-vs-bias decision rule for depth L.
