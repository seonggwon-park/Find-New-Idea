# P04 prior-art check: SIC-aware WUSH (build the transform from Schur-complement pivot blocks)

**Verdict: survives_narrowed. Novelty 3/5.**
Part of the "verbatim transfer" premise is false for WUSH's own setting (W4A4). The proposed change is one line in WUSH's published Algorithm 2.

## Key finding: WUSH already computes D_g and uses it, but not for building the transform

WUSH (2512.00956, v2 updated 2026-05-30), Algorithm 2, "GPTQ case". Page 3, read with `pdftotext -layout`:

- `MX(i) i ∈ {1..din/d} ← diagonal blocks of H` with the comment "second moment of activations (same as the RTN case)". The transform is built from the **marginal** H_ii.
- `LL⊤ ← Cholesky (H + λ…I)^{-1}` with the comment "Cholesky of Hessian inverse (same as GPTQ)".
- `Twush(i) ← WUSH(MW(i), MX(i), λ)`, where MW(i) is the second moment of the *feedback-updated* weight block.
- `H(i) ← Twush(i) (L(i,i) L(i,i)^T)^{-1} Twush(i)^T` with the comment "transformed Hessian". This is the intra-block GPTQ Hessian.
- `W(j) ← W(j) + L(j,i) L(i,i)^{-1} E(i)` is the inter-block feedback.

Here L is the lower Cholesky factor of H^{-1}. Then (H_{≥i,≥i})^{-1} = L_{≥i,≥i} L_{≥i,≥i}^T, so (L_ii L_ii^T)^{-1} = H_ii − H_{i,>i} H_{>i,>i}^{-1} H_{>i,i} = D_i. That is exactly the candidate's pivot block.

So WUSH already computes D_i and feeds it to GPTQ as the intra-block Hessian. It only uses the marginal H_ii when it **builds** T. The candidate's change replaces `MX(i) ← H_ii` with `MX(i) ← (L_ii L_ii^T)^{-1}`, a quantity Algorithm 2 already has. WUSH also already couples the transform to the processing order through MW(i), because MW(i) is computed from the updated weights.

## What overlaps

- **WUSH Alg. 2:** the block-LDL pivot (L_ii L_ii^T)^{-1} is already computed and used as GPTQ's block Hessian, and the transform is already order-coupled through the updated MW(i). The paper does not state the delta.
- **SchurQuant 2608.15567 (2026-08-16):** uses Schur-complement curvature for group-level decisions, namely scale/zero-point refit plus integer codes. Lines 69–70: "coordinate descent evaluates the group under the raw block Gcc … although that suffix can absorb part of the error. The exact curvature remaining after its best continuous response is the Schur complement". This is the same insight (use the pivot, not the marginal, for per-group choices), applied to grid and codes instead of the transform.
- **CurveTQ 2610.09212:** D_b, diagonal only, in the Viterbi metric.
- **Ordentlich–Polyanskiy 2605.13768:** GPTQ = SIC (Lemma 1). The WaterSIC rate gap is the AM/GM of the pivots, and rotation moves the pivots' AM toward their GM. This is scalar, entropy-coded, and not AbsMax. In the MIMO literature, the GMD/UCD precoder plus SIC (and V-BLAST ordering) is the classical "joint transform + SIC ordering" solution for equal-rate streams. Adjacent prior art for the framing, though not for the AbsMax block objective.
- **Chen et al. 2507.18553:** GPTQ = Babai. Basis reduction is left to future work (line 876: "exploring (scale-aware) basis reductions").

## What remains (not found anywhere)

1. A statement and proof that, under block SIC with block size equal to the group size, the deployed AbsMax distortion is Σ_g E‖e_g‖²_{D_g}. Also that block-diagonal congruence equivariance makes the WUSH optimum, with Σ_gg → D_g, the exact optimum for **weight-only** AbsMax plus error feedback within the stochastic model.
2. For W4A4: the asymmetric bilateral problem, with weight error weighted by D_g and activation error from Σ_gg. Its optimum (closed form or provable bound) is unknown. Nobody has solved it.
3. Group ordering as a joint variable with T. The optimum depends on the order through D_g. Ordering heuristics (det D_g, or the AM/GM of within-group pivots after the transform) have not been tested against WUSH+GPTQ.
4. An empirical test that the gain scales with inter-group correlation, e.g. ‖Σ_gg − D_g‖/‖Σ_gg‖ per layer.

## Sharpened hypothesis

For **weight-only** per-group AbsMax quantization with block error feedback (GPTQ/LDLQ, block size = group size):

- Under WUSH's stochastic model, the exactly optimal block-diagonal transform is WUSH built from the pivot blocks D_g = (L_gg L_gg^T)^{-1} instead of H_gg. Prove this.
- Show that the expected gain over WUSH-from-marginals is controlled by the per-group discrepancy between D_g and H_gg in the WUSH functional. Measure that discrepancy per layer.
- For **W4A4**, derive the asymmetric bilateral optimum (D_g weighting the weight error, Σ_gg driving the activation error). Show when it beats both WUSH and pivot-WUSH.
- Choose the group order jointly with T by a pivot-based criterion, and report which part of any gain comes from the order and which from the transform.

## Significance

A main-track reviewer would see the weight-only part as a small, correct refinement: a one-line change to WUSH Alg. 2 using a quantity it already computes. It is publishable only with a clear gain at 3–4 bits.

The W4A4 asymmetric derivation and the ordering-plus-transform question touch the survey's open problem 1 more directly. They are what would make it a paper.

The risk is that the gain is small. WUSH's own GPTQ margin over Hadamard is only +0.7 points (abstract line 35), and GPTQ's feedback already absorbs much of the cross-group correlation.
