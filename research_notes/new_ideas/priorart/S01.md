# S01 prior-art check: posterior-quantile drafts for quantized self-speculative decoding

**Verdict: survives_narrowed. Novelty 2/5.** No paper shapes a draft from its own logit-noise posterior. But the theorem is the textbook capacitated multi-product newsvendor, and two claims in the hypothesis do not hold: the "first-order" claim and the ">=2% accepted length" prediction.

## Premise checks (all premises hold)
- **2610.00694 l.36:** "total variation measured under teacher forcing predicts greedy draft acceptance with a mean relative error of 1.1–2.4%". Correct as cited.
  - L.308-314 also says: "total variation has been used directly as a training loss for generation and for drafts (Ji et al., 2023; Goel et al., 2024); we study it as an evaluation statistic."
  - L.435: the sign of quantization shifts at fragile positions is about 50/50 (median 0.504). This fits the zero-mean noise model.
- **2605.02404 l.302-307:** EAR = sum min(p,q) = 1 − TV, used as a fidelity metric. Correct. It is a metric only and does not shape any draft.
- **2605.26266:** a per-key heteroscedastic mean-bias correction in attention softmax. The paper says "second-order Taylor ... log(sinh α/α) ≈ α²/6" (l.367). Correct that it targets the mean, not TV. Its form, a per-element variance-dependent multiplicative reweighting, is close to S01's.
- **2608.14191:** first- and second-order softmax expansion for KV distortion, not TV. Correct.
- **2610.10411 (EDR), 2609.24150 (WTV/EAL losses), 2602.05774 (VSD), 2403.00858 (TV distillation):**
  - All train drafters to maximize acceptance or TV overlap.
  - None models observation noise. Correct as cited.
  - **Conceptual overlap:** a drafter trained on E[TV] when it cannot tell contexts apart learns exactly the Bayes act for TV loss, which is the constrained-quantile solution. The theorem is the population minimizer of a loss that is already published. It is just never written out as a quantile rule.

## The math core is not new
**This is the capacitated multi-product newsvendor.**
- Maximize E sum_i min(D_i, q_i) subject to sum q_i = C.
- The Lagrangian/KKT condition gives the equal-critical-fractile rule Pr[D_i > q_i] = λ, i.e. q_i = F_i^{-1}(1−λ).
- This is textbook operations research (Hadley & Whitin 1963; Lau & Lau 1996; budget variants such as arXiv 2301.02662).
- With demand D_i := P(x) and capacity 1, it is S01's Theorem.
- What is new is only the application, not the theorem.

## Problems with the premises (from simulation)
Script: `scratchpad/s01sim/sim.py` and `sim2.py`. Gaussian logit noise, flat prior, exact quantile computed by Monte Carlo.

**1. The correction is second order, not first order.**
- The solved quantile level u* = 1−λ is 0.51–0.60.
- Reason: to first order, the posterior median of each P(x) is p̂(x), and the medians sum to 1.
- So z_λ = O(s), and the correction exp(−z_λ s_x) = 1 − O(s²). That is the same order as the −σ²/2 mean fix the hypothesis says it differs from.

**2. "Zero under homoscedastic noise" is misleading.**
- With isotropic logit noise σ, the delta-method std is s_x = σ·sqrt(1 − 2p(x) + Σp²). That is not constant.
- The corollary is about s_x, not about the logit noise. It is also only exact under the lognormal approximation.

**3. Gains are tiny in the regime where self-drafts are useful.**

Moderate heterogeneity (σ_x ∈ [0.2, 1.8]·σ):

| σ | acceptance (p̂) | gain of q* over best temperature (absolute) | share of rejection mass recovered |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.3 | 0.914 | +0.0002 | 0.15% |
| 1.0 | 0.765 | +0.0015 | 0.64% |

Extreme heterogeneity (σ_x spread 10–20×):

| acceptance | gain (absolute) | share of rejection mass recovered |
|---|---|---|
| 0.82 | +0.0013 | 0.7% |
| 0.67 | +0.007 | 2.1% |
| 0.46 | +0.0175 | 3.2% |

**Effect on accepted length (γ=4):**
- At α≈0.67, +0.007 acceptance gives about +1.3% accepted length.
- A ≥2% gain needs α≲0.5 together with σ spread of ≥10×. Self-drafts in that regime are not worth running.
- The prediction "≥2% mean accepted length at W2/W3/KV2" is probably false in practice.
- The idealized gain is also an upper bound. Real use loses more from per-token σ_x estimation error and non-Gaussian noise.

**4. The greedy part is nearly empty.**
- For jointly Gaussian logits, Pr[z_a > z_b] > 1/2 exactly when mean(z_a) > mean(z_b).
- So the argmax of Pr[x is argmax] can differ from argmax ẑ only when ≥3 near-tied candidates have very different variances.

**5. The delta-method closed form gets almost all of q*'s gain.**
- The tuned delta-method form p̂·exp(−z·s_x) reaches 0.6746 vs q*'s 0.6756 in the first extreme-heterogeneity case (acceptance 0.67).
- A simple normalized posterior median comes close to q* at small σ.
- The method therefore reduces to a variance-dependent reweighting with one tuned scalar. That is the same form as the 2605.26266-style per-element corrections.

## What overlaps and what remains
**Overlaps:**
- The KKT/quantile theorem is the capacitated newsvendor.
- 1−TV as acceptance is published (Leviathan; 2605.02404; 2610.00694).
- TV-trained drafters implicitly learn this Bayes act (2403.00858, 2609.24150, 2610.10411).
- Per-element, variance-dependent softmax corrections exist (2605.26266).

**Remains:**
- No one has written the speculative-decoding draft as the Bayes act under a known quantization-noise posterior. That includes the joint choice of (draft bit-width, γ) using the noise model.

**Significance for a main-track reviewer:** low. The theorem is a known OR result. The effect is second order, and its size (about 0.1–1% accepted length in realistic regimes) is within run-to-run noise. A main-track reviewer would at most accept it as a remark inside a broader paper on quantized self-speculation.

## Sharpened hypothesis (if pursued at all)
Restate it as:
- **Negative/bounding result:** under per-token Gaussian logit noise with std s_x, any training-free reshaping of a quantized self-draft (temperature, mean, median or quantile corrections) can gain at most O(E_x[p(x) Var(s)·s]) in acceptance, which is second order in s.
- **Planning use:** acceptance ≈ 1 − c·E[p·s], so the noise model selects the draft bit-width and γ jointly.

Test plan:
- Measure s_x from paired logits for W2/W3/KV2 drafts.
- Report the bound against the realized gains.
- Use the noise model as an offline planner for (bits, γ), compared with measured vLLM throughput.
